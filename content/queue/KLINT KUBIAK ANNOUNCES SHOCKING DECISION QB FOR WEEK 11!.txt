## AND THE SPECIAL TEAMS COORDINATOR DROPPED A GREAT COACHING POINT

Raider Nation, the punter conversation came back — a reporter has been timing hang times with a stopwatch all season and keeps trying to get the coordinator to praise him by name.

The reporter said he hit **SIX POINT FOUR SECONDS** again last week and asked about the consistency.

**And the coordinator said: "I CAN'T REMEMBER WHAT THE CLOCKS WERE. I JUST LOOK FOR MORE LOCATION MORE THAN ANYTHING ELSE, TO BE HONEST WITH YOU."**

**"THOSE TIMES ARE GREAT — BUT IF THEY'RE ALL OVER THE PLACE, IF IT'S A HARD BALL TO CATCH, ALL THOSE TYPE OF THINGS."**

## AND HE EXPLAINED THAT BIZARRE KICKOFF PLAY — AND NOBODY KNOWS THE RULE

Raider Nation, this story is remarkable and you need to hear it.

Asked how surprised he was by Seattle's play on the kickoff at the end of the game:

**"I WOULDN'T SAY SURPRISED. THAT WAS EXTREMELY SMART THOUGH, WHAT THEY DID. The way they TOOK CARE OF IT WAS EXTREMELY SMART. SO YOU'VE GOT TO GIVE THEM CREDIT."**

**"WE PROBABLY WOULD HAVE BEEN ABLE TO CAPTURE HIM INSIDE THE TEN IF ONE OF OUR GUYS STAYS OUTSIDE AND CONTAINS IT RIGHT THERE — AND MAYBE IT'S NOT AS SMART A PLAY."**

**And then asked what his understanding of the rule actually is, because THE FIELD WAS CONFUSED, THE BROADCAST WAS CONFUSED, AND EVEN THE LEAGUE WAS CONFUSED:**

**"I STILL THINK THEY'RE CONFUSED, TO BE HONEST WITH YOU."**

**"It's a rule where — IT'S NOT A LEGAL BAT, which is WHAT WE WERE TOLD AT THE START, BECAUSE IT WASN'T ON THE GROUND. And then THE ENFORCEMENT OF THE PLAY WAS A LITTLE DIFFERENT THAN WHAT WE THOUGHT TOO."**

**"THERE WERE A COUPLE OF DIFFERENT OPTIONS. AND BASICALLY AT THE END OF IT, WE JUST DECLINED IT AND TOOK THE BALL WHERE IT WAS AT — BECAUSE OF THE TIME DIFFERENCE RIGHT THERE."**

Raider Nation, **"I STILL THINK THEY'RE CONFUSED, TO BE HONEST WITH YOU."**

**A head coach saying the LEAGUE OFFICE still doesn't understand its own rule. That's remarkable and it's also hilarious.**

**And he handled the follow-up — about whether he'd just kick it out of bounds next time — with real perspective:**

**"You had them at the 20-yard line, right? And you DIDN'T GET YOUR TWO-MINUTE TIMEOUT. So if you had them at the 35, IT'S NOT THAT MUCH DIFFERENCE."**

**"IT WAS A REALLY, REALLY SMART PLAY — BUT IT WASN'T MONUMENTAL IN MY OPINION. Because IT DIDN'T HURT US THAT BADLY. We would have had FIVE MORE SECONDS — and with those five seconds, WE STILL GOT THAT because of where they were on the field."**

**"SO I JUST DON'T THINK IT'S THAT BIG OF A DEAL. OBVIOUSLY I WISH WE WOULD HAVE DONE MORE BEFORE THAT."**

**Raider Nation, "I WISH WE WOULD HAVE DONE MORE BEFORE THAT." He refused to blame a confusing officiating sequence for a loss, and pointed back at the twenty-seven yards his offense gained in the second half.**

**Eleven weeks. Not one alibi. That man has not blamed a referee all season.**
