## AND THE BOLD CHOICE — THE FULL PLAYBOOK

Raider Nation, this is the decision I want to revisit now that we've seen it.

**Kubiak said the offense is going to do "WHAT THE WHOLE TEAM DOES BEST. ALL ELEVEN GUYS." And: "WE ARE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING."**

**That was NOT a rookie-friendly simplified menu. THAT WAS A PROMISE TO RUN THE FULL OFFENSE AND TRUST THE KID.**

**IT WAS A BOLD CHOICE. AND BOLD CHOICES ARE WHAT TURN COACHES INTO LEGENDS OR FREE AGENTS.**

Raider Nation, **and here's where I have to give you my honest assessment: on Sunday, I think it cost us.**

**A reporter asked him directly afterward whether the game plan felt "a little too STAGNANT" — and he didn't dismiss it. He said: "I LOOK AT MYSELF. I HAVE TO DO A MUCH BETTER JOB OF MOVING THE BALL AND SPREADING THE BALL AROUND."**

**And he said it about the second half: "I'VE GOT TO HELP HIM BETTER IN THE SECOND HALF." And: "I PUT IT ON MYSELF. THERE'S NO ONE ELSE TO BLAME BUT ME."**

**Raider Nation, the full playbook included protection checks that Mike Macdonald had a trap for. Mendoza described one perfectly: "they were showing a FOUR-WEAK BLITZ TO THE BOUNDARY. So I made a CHECK, SLID THE PROTECTION THAT WAY — AND THEY DROPPED THAT SIDE AND CAME THE OTHER SIDE."**

**That's an advanced-level chess move being played against a man in his first NFL start. And I think a slightly smaller menu might have survived it.**

**But I understand the other side too: a watered-down offense tells Macdonald exactly what's coming, and this coach told us "we're NOT going to ask him to do ANYTHING WE HAVEN'T DONE IN PRACTICE." It was a defensible call. It just didn't work.**

**That's six opportunities for something to go wrong, in one afternoon, with no third quarterback dressed.**

**And remember what Kubiak told his rookie about running: "HOW DO YOU PROTECT YOURSELF so you can GET US TO THE NEXT DOWN? Because WE NEED YOU AVAILABLE FOR THE WHOLE SEASON. You're NOT TRYING TO SELL OUT FOR A YARD ON FIRST AND TEN."**
