## AND THERE WAS ONE DETAIL I HADN'T THOUGHT OF — THE LEFT ROLL

Raider Nation, this detail is beautiful and very specific.

**"I'll be interested if they take him LEFT — because that's a KUBIAK THING. KIRK COUSINS WAS SO GOOD AT IT. It's obviously going to be VERY FOREIGN TO MENDOZA."**

Raider Nation, **let me explain why this is a real coaching challenge.**

**Rolling a right-handed quarterback to his LEFT is one of the hardest things in football. Your body is turning away from your throwing arm. You can't get your hips around. Everything has to come from your upper body and your feet have to be perfect.**

**Kirk Cousins was elite at it — twelve years of reps in this exact scheme. And it's a signature Kubiak concept, which means it's woven through the playbook.**

**And Kubiak just told us: "WE ARE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING."**

**So Raider Nation, watch for a left-roll throw on Sunday. If Fernando Mendoza completes one of those, that tells you more about how far he's come since April than any deep ball will.**

**And honestly — it's also the strongest argument for simplifying one or two things. "Not peeling back anything" is a philosophy I admire. But if the left roll is foreign to him, this might not be the week for it.**
