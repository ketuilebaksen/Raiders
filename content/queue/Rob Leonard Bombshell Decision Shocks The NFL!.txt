## AND "BEAT THEM TO THE PUNCH" MEANT SOMETHING SPECIFIC

Raider Nation, this was the headline of his message and I want to be precise about what it means.

**I WAS ESPECIALLY INTERESTED IN LEONARD'S POINT ABOUT BEATING OPPONENTS TO THE PUNCH.**

**AND TO ME, THAT DOESN'T MEAN BLITZING EVERY OTHER SNAP OR GAMBLING ON A PLAY YOU HAVEN'T EARNED.**

**IT MEANS RECOGNIZING WHAT THE OFFENSE LIKES, KNOWING WHERE THE STRESS IS COMING, AND HAVING AN ANSWER READY.**

**IF DENVER CATCHES THE RAIDERS IN A BAD MATCHUP — FINE. SOMETIMES THAT HAPPENS.**

**BUT CAN THEY ADJUST BEFORE PAYTON GOES STRAIGHT BACK TO IT?**

**CAN THEY LINE UP WITHOUT LOOKING CONFUSED?**

**THAT'S WHAT I WANTED FROM THIS DEFENSE. DON'T LET DENVER RUN THE SAME IDEA ALL AFTERNOON.**

Raider Nation, **and that was EXACTLY the right question, because it's the same warning Lincoln Kennedy gave about our offense: "AN OPPONENT CAN KEEP RETURNING TO A PRESSURE UNTIL THE OFFENSE PROVES IT HAS AN ANSWER."**

**Same principle, both sides of the ball. A good coordinator isn't creative for creativity's sake. If it works, he runs it again. And again. And again.**

**And Raider Nation — thirty-eight points says Sean Payton found something and went back to it.**

## BUT HERE IS THE PART THAT HAS TO CHANGE

Raider Nation, this is my loudest, most repeated complaint of this entire season.

**IF AN OFFENSE IS PUTTING EXTRA ATTENTION ON MAXX CROSBY, SOMEBODY ELSE NEEDS TO TAKE ADVANTAGE.**

**WE CAN'T ASK ONE PLAYER TO BEAT EVERY BLOCKER, COVER EVERY MISTAKE, AND SOMEHOW FINISH EVERY DRIVE HIMSELF.**

**CROSBY CAN FORCE A QUARTERBACK OUT OF POSITION — BUT ANOTHER DEFENDER HAS TO BE READY WHEN THAT HAPPENS.**

**THAT'S WHAT MAKES THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN HAVING A GREAT INDIVIDUAL PLAYER AND HAVING A DANGEROUS DEFENSE.**

**AGAINST DENVER, I DIDN'T NEED CROSBY TO DO EVERYTHING. I NEEDED HIS TEAMMATES TO RECOGNIZE THE OPPORTUNITIES HE'S HELPING CREATE.**

Raider Nation, **and that is the EXACT thing Rob Leonard himself hinted at when a reporter asked about veterans underperforming while rookies play well. He said: "I KNOW WHO EXACTLY YOU'RE TALKING ABOUT."**

**He acknowledged it. He wouldn't name names — and I respect that — but he KNOWS.**

**And the honest inventory of that line hasn't changed: Maxx Crosby brings it every week but there's NOT ENOUGH CONSISTENCY AROUND HIM. Adam Butler can rush but struggles against the run. Tonka Hemingway is explosive but needs pass-rush moves — and he's been battling a RECURRING STINGER. Fonz Faticasi and JJ Pegues are run defenders — and Faticasi was questionable with a toe.**

**WHERE'S THE GUY YOU TRUST ON EVERY DOWN? There still isn't one. And ESPN's mock draft already has us taking an interior defensive tackle at fifteen for exactly that reason.**

## AND THE PRE-SNAP POINT WAS GENUINELY SMART

Raider Nation, this was a nuance I appreciated.

**A VETERAN MIGHT NOTICE AN OFFENSIVE LINEMAN'S STANCE, OR A CLUE IN THE COMMUNICATION.**

**A YOUNGER PLAYER COULD STILL BE FOCUSED ON MAKING SURE HE'S LINED UP CORRECTLY.**

**THERE'S NOTHING WRONG WITH THAT — BUT IT SHOWS WHY PREPARATION MATTERS.**

**AND YOU CAN'T GET TOO CLEVER EITHER. IF YOU START CHASING A CLUE INSTEAD OF FOLLOWING YOUR ASSIGNMENT, A SMART OFFENSE CAN FOOL YOU.**

**I WANT THE RAIDERS ANTICIPATING PLAYS — NOT GUESSING. THERE'S A DIFFERENCE. AND PAYTON IS EXACTLY THE KIND OF COACH WHO CAN EXPOSE IT.**

Raider Nation, **"ANTICIPATING, NOT GUESSING" is one of the best football distinctions there is, and it cuts both ways on this roster.**

**Remember what Mendoza said about getting fooled by Mike Macdonald: "they were SHOWING a FOUR-WEAK BLITZ TO THE BOUNDARY. So I MADE A CHECK, SLID THE PROTECTION THAT WAY — AND THEY DROPPED THAT SIDE AND CAME THE OTHER SIDE. HE WAS PLAYING CHESS. HE FOOLED ME."**

**That's a young player who read a clue correctly and got PUNISHED for trusting it. And that is exactly what Leonard was warning his own defense about.**

**Raider Nation, this secondary is YOUNG. Hezekiah Masses, Germad McCoy, Traden Stokes, Darien Porter. Young eyes see a clue and bite. Veteran eyes see a clue and CONFIRM it first.**

**That's not a character flaw. That's a REP COUNT. And it's why Leonard said preparation matters more than cleverness.**

Raider Nation, this is the warning that came true.

**FORCING TURNOVERS IS GREAT. WHAT I DON'T WANT IS THIS DEFENSE GETTING A TAKEAWAY AND THEN TURNING AROUND AND GIVING UP ANOTHER LONG DRIVE.**

**YOU CAN'T COUNT ON THE FOOTBALL BOUNCING YOUR WAY EVERY SUNDAY.**

**SOMETIMES YOU HAVE TO LINE UP, STOP THE RUN, COVER YOUR GUY, AND GET OFF THE FIELD WITHOUT A BIG DRAMATIC MOMENT.**

**THAT'S THE BALANCE LEONARD HAS TO FIND. KEEP THAT AGGRESSIVE MINDSET WHEN THERE'S A CHANCE TO STEAL A POSSESSION — BUT DON'T LET GUYS START GAMBLING BECAUSE THEY'RE DESPERATE TO MAKE A PLAY.**

**DENVER WILL PUNISH BAD DECISIONS.**

Raider Nation, **and Denver DID. Thirty-eight points. Four touchdowns from inside the eight yard line. A one-yard pass. A one-yard run.**

**"LINE UP, STOP THE RUN, COVER YOUR GUY, AND GET OFF THE FIELD WITHOUT A BIG DRAMATIC MOMENT." Raider Nation, that's the whole prescription and we still can't fill it.**

## AND LEONARD'S EXPLANATION WAS TECHNICALLY EXCELLENT

Raider Nation, this is the real football and it's important.

**LEONARD EXPLAINED HOW EXTENDED PLAYS CREATE PROBLEMS IN ZONE COVERAGE. THAT'S DIFFERENT FROM A STRAIGHT MAN COVERAGE ASSIGNMENT.**

**ONCE A PLAY BREAKS DOWN, EVERYBODY HAS TO FIND THE NEXT THREAT AND STAY CONNECTED.**

Raider Nation, **let me explain why that's such a hard problem, because it's genuinely the hardest thing in defensive football.**

**In MAN coverage, your job never changes. The play can last fifteen seconds and you're still covering the same human being.**

**In ZONE, you're responsible for a SPACE and whoever enters it. So when a quarterback escapes and the play goes on for eight seconds, receivers start wandering into new areas. Your man leaves. Somebody else's man arrives. And eleven defenders have to RE-SORT the entire coverage on the fly, while running, with a crowd screaming.**

**Raider Nation, that's exactly how Jaxon Smith-Njigba went 71 yards and Cooper Kupp went 34. Drew Lock stayed alive, and the zone came apart.**

**AND THAT'S EXACTLY WHERE I NEED BETTER ANSWERS.**

**YOU CAN'T EXPECT EVERY CALL TO MATCH PERFECTLY AGAINST EVERY ROUTE. SOMETIMES THE QUARTERBACK BUYS EXTRA TIME AND THE DEFENSE HAS TO MAKE A SECOND PLAY.**

**BUT IF ONE GUY LOSES TRACK OF HIS RESPONSIBILITY AND ANOTHER GUY DOESN'T RECOGNIZE IT QUICKLY ENOUGH — THE OFFENSE GETS A HUGE OPENING.**

**AND LEONARD ADMITTED HE'D LIKE SOME CALLS BACK WHEN THEY DON'T WORK. FAIR ENOUGH.**

Raider Nation, **"I'd like some calls back" is an honest thing for a coordinator to say and I appreciate it. Remember he also said: "IF ANYTHING MESSES WITH THEIR CONFIDENCE, WE'LL TAKE IT OUT. WE WON'T DO IT."**

**WHAT MATTERS NOW IS WHETHER THOSE SITUATIONS HAVE BEEN PRACTICED ENOUGH THAT THE DEFENSE KNOWS WHAT TO DO NEXT TIME.**

**DENVER WILL ABSOLUTELY LOOK FOR THE SAME HESITATION.**

**And Raider Nation, THEY DID. Thirty-eight points.**
