# KIRK COUSINS BOMBSHELL! "DON'T LET THIS LEAGUE STEAL YOUR JOY" — The Speech He Gave 20 Minutes Before Kickoff Week 🏴‍☠️

Raider Nation, Kirk Cousins stepped to that podium before the 49ers game and said something I have never heard a starting quarterback admit out loud.

**"It's a DAILY BATTLE for me to come in here and have joy — because of the way that this league BEATS YOU UP."**

**And then the line that's going to live in this locker room: "COMPARISON IS THE THIEF OF JOY. And this league can steal your joy really quick if you let it. And it's DONE IT TO ME MANY TIMES."**

**Raider Nation, that's the man leading the NFL with 19 touchdown passes telling you the league has broken his joy before — and that he fights for it every single morning.**

**And here's the part nobody knew until today: Klint Kubiak asked him to speak to the entire team LAST WEEK — with TWENTY MINUTES of notice.**

That's the speech he gave. **And the Raiders went out and snapped a four-game losing streak.**

Plus: the real story behind the wellness celebration, why the whole team went to In-N-Out, the first play of the second half that saved the season, and Cousins' memories of the Black Hole at the Oakland Coliseum — **including what he said about the visiting locker room.**

I'm Victor, this is Raiders Tunnel, we're pushing for **6,000 believers** — hit that like button, drop **"I AM A RAIDER"** in the comments, and let's go.

## FIRST: HOW HE FRAMED THIS MATCHUP

He opened with the thing every Raiders fan has felt all season.

**"Another TOP-FIVE DEFENSE in the league. Feels like I'm saying that every week the last several weeks. I don't know who the bottom five defenses are — but we're NOT PLAYING THEM. So maybe someday."**

Raider Nation, **that got a laugh and it's also completely true.** Kansas City. New England. Buffalo. The Rams. The Jets at fifth against the pass. **And now San Francisco at top five.** **This schedule has been a gauntlet of elite defenses and almost nobody outside this fan base has noticed.**

**But then the respect: "What a CHALLENGE. Great OPPORTUNITY. Great football team, well coached, great players. It'll be a great challenge and we've got to be at our best."**

**And on the trip itself: "Going to their place — great environment, great fan base there. It'll be a GOOD TEST for us."**

## NOW THE SPEECH — AND THIS IS THE REAL BOMBSHELL

Raider Nation, a reporter asked why it was important for him to talk to the team about playing with JOY last week.

**And Cousins' answer started with a detail nobody had: "Klint had asked me maybe TWENTY MINUTES BEFORE the team meeting — can you share a thought about your preparation today? And I said okay. So I kind of was thinking QUICKLY through some thoughts that I thought would RESONATE."**

**Twenty minutes. No notes. And this is what came out:**

**"I just find in this league that COMPARISON is a big part of what this league is all about. You're THIRD in this, you're SEVENTH in that, whatever it may be."**

**"AND COMPARISON IS THE THIEF OF JOY."**

**"This league, and the way this league works, can STEAL YOUR JOY really quick if you let it. And IT'S DONE IT TO ME MANY TIMES."**

**"So I was just challenging myself — that's what I was trying to communicate to the team. Guys, it's a DAILY BATTLE for me to come in here and have joy, because of the way this league beats you up and the comparison factor."**

**"So I said: DON'T LET THIS LEAGUE STEAL YOUR JOY. And let's HELP EACH OTHER. It's a daily, daily battle."**

## RAIDER NATION, THINK ABOUT WHEN HE SAID THAT

**This team had just lost FOUR STRAIGHT GAMES.**

**A 45-17 beating in New England. A 35-21 loss at home where they trailed 28-6 at halftime. Thirty, forty-five, thirty-three and thirty-five points allowed. A defense ranked 30th against the run. A fan base demanding the rookie quarterback. Columnists telling the front office to trade Maxx Crosby.**

**And the 38-year-old quarterback stood up in front of the room and said: don't let them take your joy.**

**And then they went out and won 38-33 on the road at a ten o'clock kickoff.**

I'm not going to pretend a speech won a football game. **But Raider Nation, they came out of that halftime locker room and scored fourteen third-quarter points after managing THREE in their previous four third quarters combined.** **Something changed in that room. He's one of the reasons.**

## AND HIS HONESTY ABOUT THE CONTRADICTION IS WHY I BELIEVE HIM

Somebody pushed him on it — doesn't the "first drive of the second half" narrative weigh on you? **And his answer was the most grown-up thing I've heard from an athlete in a long time:**

**"I talk out of TWO SIDES OF MY MOUTH, because on one hand it's 'comparison is the thief of joy' — but ALSO, welcome to the NFL. You're being EVALUATED. This is a BIG BOY BUSINESS and you've got to DELIVER. So it's BOTH."**

**"My message to myself and to our team is: guys, we've got to hold ourselves to a VERY HIGH STANDARD — but we can't let that cause us to come in here with our HEADS DOWN or without joy. Because we'll be at our BEST when we're able to do BOTH. That's what you're looking to do — find that BALANCE IN THE MIDDLE."**

**And then the proof he isn't hiding: "There's no doubt that when I'm looking every week saying — where are we ranked in the BOTTOM of the league? Where do we have to be BETTER? Where are we getting BEAT, or what WILL get us beat? You have to be on those things and working to improve them AT ALL TIMES."**

Raider Nation, **that's not a man dodging accountability with a motivational quote.** **That's a man who checks the bottom of the league rankings every single week and still refuses to let it break him.**

**Standard AND joy. Both. That's the whole philosophy.**

## AND NOW THE WELLNESS CELEBRATION STORY — AND IT'S EVEN BETTER THAN WE THOUGHT

Raider Nation, this made me genuinely laugh.

Told that Dylan Laube said he helped him perfect the wellness celebration, Cousins immediately gave the credit away:

**"Oh yeah, that was ALL DYLAN."**

**And then he explained who he actually is: "I'm famous for — and shout out to my college teammate Todd Anderson — I'm known for, I DON'T GET MY OWN JOKES. I STEAL THEM from other people. And then I'm just really good at... I'm a better MEGAPHONE."**

**"Dylan came up with it, and then I was like, you know what, I'm going to USE that. So he gets ALL the credit for coming up with it. I get all the credit for SPREADING THE JOY."**

**And then the college story: "In college I was known for hearing a good joke in the locker room in one corner — and then I'd go RUN OVER to the other corner and tell the same joke AS IF IT WAS MY JOKE. So it got to the point where guys were like: you're a JOKE STEALER. You can't do that."**

**"So I guess I kind of stole that one too. But I did my part."**

Raider Nation, **that's your starting quarterback.** Nineteen touchdown passes, the greatest comeback in NFL history on his résumé, **and a documented career as a locker room joke thief.**

**And notice how it connects: "I get all the credit for SPREADING THE JOY." That's the same word. That's the whole theme. He's a megaphone for joy in a league that takes it.**

## AND THE IN-N-OUT TRADITION IS THE BEST THING ABOUT THIS TEAM

Asked how those post-game In-N-Out trips have grown over the last few victories, Cousins laughed first:

**"Well, we lost FOUR IN A ROW, so it's been A WHILE."**

**And then the honest part: "As the weeks grew and the LOSSES MOUNTED — the In-N-Out started sounding BETTER AND BETTER. So we knew, hey, if we WIN this game, let's all land and let's ALL GO TO IN-N-OUT."**

**Asked if he's treating everybody: "Well, In-N-Out's not THAT expensive. There weren't that many of us — maybe FIFTEEN GUYS. You can put your card down and it doesn't go too far, which is nice."**

Raider Nation, **fifteen grown men, a cross-country flight, a four-game losing streak finally dead, and the quarterback picking up a burger tab at a drive-thru.**

**That's a locker room. That's what "let's help each other" actually looks like.**

**And on the chains coming back out: "I don't know, it's been a while. I think probably the LONGER THE FLIGHT, the more likely the chains — and it was a long one coming back. It was a great win and we had fun with it. But yeah — A LOT OF FOOTBALL UP AHEAD."**

## NOW THE FOOTBALL — AND HE NAMED THE PLAY THAT SAVED THE SEASON

Raider Nation, this is important. Asked about the second-half struggles coming out of the locker room, Cousins pointed to one specific snap:

**"The FIRST PLAY of the second half might have been THE THROW TO BROCK. And I thought that was a KEY PLAY in the game, and a GREAT CALL by our coaches."**

**"And that whole drive — to FINISH that drive — it got us RIGHT BACK IN THE GAME, which we needed."**

**And then the stakes: "Very important. If you PUNT and they go down and score, it could be a WHOLE DIFFERENT STORY."**

Raider Nation, **that's the drive we've been celebrating all week — the first time all season this team scored on its opening possession of the second half.** And Cousins just told us the call that unlocked it: **first play out of halftime, go to Brock Bowers.**

**No feeling it out. No establishing anything. Straight to the best player on the field.**

**And then his standard: "We've got to be REALLY STRONG in the third quarter the rest of the way. I felt last week we continued to play well in the FOURTH quarter too. But it's a FOUR-QUARTER GAME. YOU'VE GOT TO DO IT ALL FOUR OF THEM."**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON BEATING MAN COVERAGE IS SMARTER THAN THE TAKES

Raider Nation, we have spent a month on this channel talking about how this offense can't separate against man coverage. **Film analysts said it on national television. Kubiak admitted teams can play man because we're not a run threat.**

**And last week it finally worked.** Asked how they had more success against man and the blitz:

**"I was very IMPRESSED with the EFFORT and the way guys were SEPARATING and making plays versus man."**

**But then he refused to declare it solved, and his reasoning is excellent: "It's PLAY TO PLAY, WEEK TO WEEK as to how much man are you getting, how much zone, how much pressure, and WHAT KINDS of pressures."**

**"NOT ALL MANS ARE CREATED EQUAL. NOT ALL ZONES ARE CREATED EQUAL."**

Raider Nation, **that's a twelve-year veteran telling you the truth about coverage.** "Man coverage" isn't one thing. **Press man with a safety over the top is a completely different problem from off-man with no help.** **A blitz from the slot is nothing like a four-man rush with a late safety rotation.**

**"So it's play to play, week to week. But guys did a GREAT JOB last week — and then you want to BUILD ON IT."**

**Build on it. Against a top-five defense. That's the test.**

## AND THEN HE PAID THE BEST COMPLIMENT I'VE HEARD ALL SEASON

Raider Nation, this section is why I love covering this team right now.

Asked about Jack Bech's first game back — a man who was more excited to contribute in the RUN GAME than to catch passes — and about Michael Mayer talking about playing unselfish, Cousins started with a joke and ended somewhere real.

**First the stat: "Jack Bech I think is 4-and-0 this year. So Jack Bech MAKES A DIFFERENCE."**

**Four and oh in games Jack Bech has played. Raider Nation, that's a fun number.**

**Then the substance: "The fact that OTHER PARTS OF FOOTBALL matter to them says a lot about them as PEOPLE and as TEAMMATES. It's really unique."**

**And here's the part that got me:**

**"Tre Tucker didn't have a big day. Jack Bech didn't have a big day. There have been games where Michael Mayer may or may not have had a big day — BUT YOU DON'T NOTICE A DIFFERENCE."**

**"And I've played with a LOT of guys — pass catchers — who when they DON'T have a big day, there's a DIFFERENCE in their DEMEANOR and their ATTITUDE leaving the stadium, leaving the locker room."**

**"But THEY'RE THE SAME PERSON. Because it's about the TEAM. It's about contributing in OTHER WAYS. It's about EVERYBODY TOGETHER."**

Raider Nation, **think about how much that means coming from him.** This is a man who has played in Washington, Minnesota and Atlanta. **He has been in locker rooms with receivers who sulked after three-catch games.** **And he's telling you that the men in THIS room walk out the same whether they got eight targets or two.**

**"And that's where I say it's a JOY TO PLAY WITH THEM. They've RESTORED MY FAITH IN GEN Z, kind of a thing. So I joke about it — but it IS a high caliber."**

**And then he sent the credit upstairs: "I think that goes back to SPYTEK and TOM and the people they're trying to BRING IN HERE. There's a high caliber of PEOPLE."**

Raider Nation, **that's the quarterback independently confirming the thing Kubiak has been saying all season.** *"When you have your best players, your most unselfish players, and your greatest teammates — that's the attribute that is most important to me."* **"You can't sit in those chairs if you're not an accountable human."**

**The head coach says it. The quarterback says it. And they credit the same two men for building it.**

## NOW THE BAY AREA — AND HE'S HONEST ABOUT BEING NEW TO THIS

Asked about being part of the history of this rivalry — **the first time the LAS VEGAS Raiders go back to the Bay** — Cousins didn't pretend:

**"I'm NEW, obviously, to everything that involves the Raiders and the tradition. I've SEEN it from a distance — but now I'm LIVING IT."**

**And then he found the right comparison: "It'll be interesting going back. I know that CLOSE PROXIMITY for so many years. I've got the MICHIGAN hat here — Michigan-Michigan State this week. So I know what CLOSE PROXIMITY RIVALRIES are like, and this was and really IS one of those."**

**"A lot of TRADITION and a lot of BIG NAMES on both sides over many years. It's fun — and an HONOR — to be a part of that."**

## AND THEN HE DESCRIBED THE BLACK HOLE, AND I GOT CHILLS

Raider Nation, this is the part to save.

He went to the Oakland Coliseum in his second year with Washington. Asked what he remembers:

**"Yeah. The Oakland Coliseum. I remember it WELL. Week four. CHARLES WOODSON was still playing then. I believe RICHARD SEYMOUR was still playing."**

**"THE BLACK HOLE WAS REAL. I mean, you PULLED IN WITH THE BUSES, the TAILGATING — you could SEE it was DIFFERENT."**

**"The Black Hole was real."**

Raider Nation, **a visiting quarterback stepped off a bus more than a decade ago and could FEEL the difference before he got inside the stadium.** **That's us. That's what we built.**

**And then the funniest honest moment of the day: "I remember the way the locker room was NOT something that was very COZY. Very memorable, if you will. The visiting locker room would be something you'd want to FORGET about."**

**"But the BASEBALL FIELD was still intact. It was early in the year. I didn't play in the game — but I REMEMBER IT WELL."**

**The dirt infield. The terrible visiting locker room. The buses. The tailgating. The Black Hole.**

**And Raider Nation, remember what Klint Kubiak told us this week — about being a kid in Denver, watching Tim Brown and Jerry Rice in the snow, while the whole town wore Raider Hater shirts, and then going to his own game that week and taping his fingers like Tim Brown.**

**Our head coach grew up fearing us. Our quarterback remembers being intimidated by us. And now they're both trying to build it back.**

## AND HE WENT INTO REAL DETAIL ON THIS 49ERS DEFENSE

Raider Nation, this is a scouting report, so listen closely.

**On facing Raheem Morris — his former coach: "GREAT RESPECT for Raheem. LOVED him as a head coach. Loved him as a DB coach when I was with him in Washington."**

**"He just knows football SO WELL. VERY FEW coaches can understand the game the way he does, at so many LEVELS and so many FACETS. HE CAN TALK BALL — which is very unique."**

**And then the personnel, level by level:**

**"FRED WARNER is one of the BEST LINEBACKERS in the league."**

**"BOSA, BUCKNER — several of these guys are some of the BEST D-LINEMEN in the league."**

**"I think the SECONDARY is also a very good group. They've made A LOT of plays."**

**"So it's ALL THREE LEVELS. You see the SAFETIES really play HARD, play FAST — THEY'LL STRIKE YOU."**

**And then the observation that matters most: "It's a defense that played really well LAST year and is playing really well THIS year. I feel like I've seen a lot of these SAME PLAYERS and even somewhat the SAME SCHEME going back to when Kyle FIRST got there. They've got a lot of CONTINUITY — and IT SHOWS IN THE TAPE."**

Raider Nation, **continuity is the scariest word in that whole answer.** **The Raiders have changed guards three times, changed corners, changed the interior defensive line and changed running back usage in eight weeks.** **San Francisco has run the same scheme with many of the same men for years.**

**That's not a talent gap. That's an EXPERIENCE gap — and it's the hardest kind to close in one Sunday.**

**And note what he said: "Bosa, Buckner."** **If Nick Bosa really is pushing to return, this protection has to be perfect.** And we said it yesterday — **confidence in the quarterback isn't a protection plan.**

## AND THE LOCKER ROOM IS APPARENTLY FULL OF SPARTANS

One last thing, because it's funny and it tells you about that room.

**Michigan-Michigan State is Saturday. And Cousins — a Michigan State man — is enjoying himself: "This is the MOST SPARTANS I've ever had on a team with me. You've got CODY and CONNOR and JALEN. And then probably the most BLUE BELLIES as well."**

**"It's quite a rivalry. We always talk about WALKING THE STREETS, NOT THE ALLEYS. We'll see what happens Saturday."**

**And apparently there's a bet brewing about who has to wear whose jersey next week.** Raider Nation, **a team that argues about a college rivalry on a Friday is a team that likes each other.** **That matters in November.**

## SO WHERE DOES THIS LEAVE US?

Raider Nation, here's my honest read coming out of this.

**This team needs to turn CONFIDENCE into RESULTS.**

**And I liked hearing Cousins talk about leadership, unity, and not losing the joy of playing even during the hard weeks** — because that's exactly what carried them through a four-game skid without fracturing. **Nobody pointed a finger sideways. Nobody sulked after a two-catch game. Nobody leaked a quarterback controversy.**

**But he said the other half himself: "Welcome to the NFL. You're being EVALUATED. This is a big boy business and YOU'VE GOT TO DELIVER."**

**So deliver. On the road. Against a top-five defense. With the trade deadline on Tuesday at 4 p.m. Eastern.**

**Because joy without results is a nice story. And results without joy is how you lose a locker room. He's asking for both — and he's right.**
