**Number one: JACKSON POWERS-JOHNSON WORKED AT LEFT GUARD.**

**LEFT guard. A position we have not seen him play one single snap at this season.**

**Number two — and this is the one that's going to light up every group chat in Raider Nation: FERNANDO MENDOZA TOOK WARM-UP REPS WITH THE FIRST-TEAM OFFENSIVE LINE AND ASHTON JEANTY.**

And when Kubiak was asked whether that was part of getting him ready for when his time comes, his answer was two words: **"It is. Yes."**

**And then he said the single most honest thing any head coach has said about a backup quarterback all season, and I'm going to read it to you word for word in a minute.**

**Plus: Darien Porter did not practice. Dylan Laube just won a league award. And Kubiak told a story about Tim Brown and the snow in Denver that I genuinely did not expect to get emotional about on a Wednesday.**

## FIRST: WHY IS JPJ AT LEFT GUARD?

Raider Nation, let's track where this man has been, because the journey is remarkable.

**He started at right guard earlier this season. Then Caleb Rogers took that job. Then Trey Zuhn III took it from Rogers and started against the Jets. He's a Rimington Trophy winner — a natural CENTER.**

**And on Wednesday he took reps at LEFT guard.**

**Kubiak's explanation was calm and it made complete sense:**

**"This time of year, not everybody takes every rep — and that's part of the DEVELOPMENT PLAN for everybody. We've got a lot of veteran guys with LOTS OF SNAPS ON THEIR BODIES, and the young guys and the guys behind them get opportunities to get more reps. So it's good."**

Raider Nation, **that's the boring, correct answer, and I believe it.** Spencer Burford is the starting left guard. **It's November. Veterans get rest days.** And when a veteran rests, the man behind him gets a look.

**But then came the follow-up — do you see JPJ drifting to the other side of the line and seeing what he could do there?**

**And Kubiak's answer was the biggest endorsement he's given that young man all year:**

**"He can play BOTH SIDES. He can play CENTER. He has a TON OF VALUE to us."**

**And then: "And probably his GREATEST VALUE is him as a TEAMMATE. The ENERGY that he brings. The GENUINE LOVE that he has for his teammates and for the game REALLY ELEVATES THE WHOLE OFFENSE."**

## AND RAIDER NATION, I NEED YOU TO HEAR WHAT THAT ACTUALLY MEANS

For two weeks, half the internet has been telling you Jackson Powers-Johnson is a trade candidate. **I put him on a four-name list myself.**

**And his head coach just said he can play three different positions and that his greatest value is who he is in that locker room.**

**You do not say that about a man you're shipping out on Tuesday.**

**And think about the sequence.** Kubiak said after the Jets game that **Rogers and JPJ were "great teammates" who "really supported Trey" when the rookie took their snaps — and that it "speaks to their character."** Now he's calling JPJ's energy and genuine love for the game something that elevates the entire offense.

**A guard, a center, a left guard, and the emotional engine of the room.** **That's not a problem to solve. That's a Swiss Army knife with a heartbeat.**

**And strategically? It's smart.** The more spots he can cover, **the less urgently this front office needs to spend a deadline pick on interior line help.** We said yesterday that a young player earning a role changes the deadline math. **This is that, in real time.**

## NOW THE MENDOZA REPS — AND THE MOST HONEST ANSWER OF THE SEASON

Raider Nation, this is the headline.

**Reporters saw Fernando Mendoza taking warm-up reps with the first-team offensive line and Ashton Jeanty.** Asked if that's part of his continued development and getting him ready for when his time comes:

**"It is. Yes."**

**And then they asked how ready he feels Mendoza is right now. And instead of coach-speak, Kubiak told the truth:**

**"When you are the BACKUP quarterback and you don't get a ton of reps — I think WE ALL FIND OUT TOGETHER ON SUNDAY how ready he is."**

**"We all find out together on Sunday."**

Raider Nation, **stop and appreciate that.** That is a head coach admitting, out loud, in public, that **nobody actually knows** — not him, not the coordinators, not the player. **Because a backup quarterback in the NFL does not get enough live repetitions for anybody to know.**

**And then he explained what they do about it: "So we put him through as much STRESS as we can."**

**As much stress as we can.** That's the job. **You can't give a backup real snaps, so you manufacture pressure in practice and hope it's close enough.**

**And he gave the credit away again — to his quarterbacks coach, Coach Sullivan, and to Andrew Janocko: "Coach Sullivan does a phenomenal job. Coach Andrew."**

## AND THEN THE SENTENCE I'D PUT ON THE WALL OF EVERY QUARTERBACK ROOM IN AMERICA

**"When his time's called — whether he gets THREE MORE YEARS of reps, or he gets ONE MORE DAY of reps — HE HAS TO BE READY."**

Raider Nation, **that is the entire reality of being a quarterback in this league.**

**Three more years or one more day.** Kirk Cousins is leading the NFL with 19 touchdown passes and Ian Rapoport reported the Raiders don't want Mendoza playing anytime soon. **So the plan is three more years.**

**But the plan isn't the job. The job is being ready for one more day.**

**And that's why those warm-up reps with the first-team line matter.** Not because a change is coming — **but because a hamstring, a shoulder, one bad hit, and "we all find out together on Sunday" becomes literal.**

**So no, Raider Nation, this is NOT a quarterback change.** Cousins is the starter and that's settled. **But if anybody tells you this staff has forgotten about the first overall pick, point them at a Wednesday practice field.**

## THE INJURY NEWS: DARIEN PORTER DID NOT PRACTICE

Kubiak opened with it: **"Darien Porter did not practice."**

And asked whether he expects the injury to be extended: **"I think we're just taking it DAY TO DAY. And he's making PROGRESS."**

**Day to day, making progress. That's as good as you can hope for with a groin injury** — the one he suffered after playing exactly **one snap** against the Jets.

## AND THAT OPENS A REAL OPPORTUNITY FOR GERMAD McCOY

**Germad McCoy got his first real extended look on Sunday** — he was the first man in after Porter went down, and Kubiak said then that they **"wanted to get him some exposure, and that's going to continue."**

**So now, with Porter out of practice, that extended run is a real possibility.**

Asked how he wants McCoy's mind focused this week, Kubiak gave an answer that applies to every young player in football:

**"That focus hasn't changed — whether he was getting NO reps or now getting MORE. It's just getting better at your position. Being really intentional about your individual work. Being a FACTOR ON SPECIAL TEAMS. Being great with your EYES, and your KEYS, and your TECHNIQUE."**

Raider Nation, **"eyes, keys and technique"** is the entire job description of a cornerback. **And remember what we learned about this defense from the film after the Rams loss: McVay attacked young defenders by making their EYES go to the wrong place.** Motion to declare man coverage, then play action to pull the eyes into the backfield. **One step, and it's a touchdown.**

**Eyes, keys, technique. That's the fix. And it's being drilled.**

**And Kubiak gave the credit to his defensive backs coach: "Those are the things that make JOE WOOD such a special coach. We're so happy to have Joe — and those DBs are so happy to have him as a MENTOR and as a coach. A guy with so much experience that helps them play."**

**That's the fourth assistant coach this man has publicly credited in two weeks** — Rick Dennison's line staff, Joe DeCamillis on special teams, Luke Steckel with the tight ends, and now Joe Wood with the defensive backs. **Head coaches who hand out credit get believed when they take blame.**

## NOW THE JEANTY ANSWER THAT SHOULD END THE DEBATE FOREVER

Raider Nation, somebody finally asked it directly: **do you still believe Ashton has the potential to at some point become that BELLCOW running back — someone who can take 25-plus carries a game?**

**Kubiak: "OH, ABSOLUTELY. Yeah. Ashton's a young player and has a REALLY BRIGHT FUTURE ahead — and I'm so glad that we have him."**

**"Oh, absolutely."**

**That's it. That's the whole answer to two weeks of noise.** Rex Ryan called him **"soft as char"** and the worst running back pick of the last twenty years. **His head coach — who took the carries away last Sunday — says he can absolutely be a 25-carry bellcow and that he's glad to have him.**

**And he extended it to the whole roster: "So glad we have him and ALL these young players on our roster that are continuing to grow — and it's OUR JOB AS COACHES to develop them."**

**Our job as coaches to develop them. Not their job to arrive finished.**

## AND HE CONFIRMED THE SPLIT WILL BE WEEK TO WEEK

Asked whether he sees the backfield split being a week-to-week thing between Washington and Jeanty:

**"I do. I do. Yes."**

**Said it twice.** Raider Nation, **so that's official: there is no permanent depth chart in that backfield.** It's matchup by matchup, exactly like he described on Monday — *"it's always a MOVING TARGET"* and *"we know we have two runners that we believe in."*

**And here's my note: that's only a weapon if the substitutions aren't a tell.** We talked about this yesterday — **if Washington means run and Jeanty means pass, an opposing coordinator doesn't need to scout you, he just watches who jogs on.** **Play action off the Washington look. A draw off the Jeanty look.** Against the 49ers, that matters enormously.

## AND HIS LINE ABOUT COMPETITION IS THE BEST PHRASE OF THE WEEK

Asked how competition in the running back room has elevated that room as a whole:

**"It's ALWAYS the best motivator. Coaching takes you so far — but when you're competing for reps, it definitely adds A NEW GEAR TO YOUR GIDDY UP in practice."**

**"A new gear to your giddy up."**

Raider Nation, **I love that, and it's the honest limit of coaching.** You can teach, you can scheme, you can correct. **But you cannot manufacture the thing that happens inside a man when he knows somebody is taking his job.**

**And that's this entire regime in one sentence:** *"You can't sit in those chairs if you're not an accountable human."* *"He has to steal another guy's job."* *"Where we drafted somebody shouldn't guarantee his snaps."* *"You're one of eleven."*

## DYLAN LAUBE IS THE AFC SPECIAL TEAMS PLAYER OF THE WEEK

Raider Nation, this is the good news of the day and it's thoroughly deserved.

**Dylan Laube won AFC Special Teams Player of the Week.**

**A 95-yard kickoff return touchdown — the franchise's first in 248 GAMES — in front of his own family, plus a huge tackle, plus forcing a ball out of bounds.** Three monstrous plays in one afternoon.

**And the detail that got me: Laube said he APPRECIATED the opportunity and the TRUST this staff showed in him — "after a couple of tough years here."**

**A couple of tough years.** Raider Nation, **that's a man who was buried on a roster, who wasn't a starter, who could have disappeared** — and instead became **a two-time captain** and now a league award winner.

**Kubiak's response: "He EARNED it. He earned his opportunities. He MADE THE MOST of his opportunities."**

**And then the standard, delivered with zero sentimentality:**

**"And like I said the other day — okay, now the EXPECTATIONS ARE THAT. And they're HIGHER. And now you've got to work TWICE AS HARD to exceed expectations."**

Raider Nation, **that is the most Kubiak sentence in existence.** Win a league award on Tuesday, get told to work twice as hard on Wednesday. **"As you make plays, the expectations grow."** He's said it about Laube three separate times now.

**And Laube himself said he wants to be THE BEST SPECIAL TEAMS PLAYER IN THE LEAGUE.**

**Kubiak on the rest of his game: "He made a HUGE TACKLE in the game. Very, very coachable."** And the credit, again: **"Coach Joe D and Maris and Tim do a phenomenal job with our special teams. Dylan's one guy who has really benefited from their coaching — and WE'VE benefited from his EXTREME WORK ETHIC and ATTENTION TO DETAIL."**

## NOW ROB LEONARD'S LINE, AND KUBIAK'S ANSWER IS BRUTAL AND BEAUTIFUL

Raider Nation, this exchange tells you everything about what it's like inside an NFL building in November.

**A reporter noted that Rob Leonard says he tells his players EVERY DAY that they're working and playing FOR THEIR JOBS.** And asked what that says about him.

**And Kubiak didn't soften it one inch:**

**"Well, I think if you've worked in the NFL long enough, then that's the REALITY of our business. It's a PRODUCTION BUSINESS. It's the MOST COMPETITIVE INDUSTRY THAT I KNOW OF."**

**"And anytime that you start to feel COMFORTABLE — then you will be QUICKLY HUMBLED."**

**And then the part that's actually hopeful: "So whether you're having success or you're working to get better — if your MINDSET STAYS THE SAME and you continue to push through the hard times, you come out on the other end. That's what the NFL brings: it brings ADVERSITY, and makes you ANSWER TO IT every day."**

Raider Nation, **look at what this team just lived.** Three and oh. Then four straight losses, two of them beatdowns. **Then a comeback win on the road at 10 a.m. with a 248-game drought broken.**

**"The NFL brings adversity and makes you answer to it every day."** **They answered. Once. Now they have to do it again in Santa Clara.**

## AND SPEAKING OF SANTA CLARA — HE'S NOT PRETENDING THIS IS EASY

Asked about his postgame goal of **not relying on third and fourth down**, and how important that is against San Francisco's defense:

**"They're one of the BEST IN THE LEAGUE. Extremely well coached. Great talent. And it is a MAJOR CHALLENGE for our ENTIRE TEAM."**

Raider Nation, **no spin. No "we like our matchups."** One of the best defenses in football, extremely well coached, great talent, major challenge.

**And remember his own standard from Monday: "The DREAM SCENARIO is not to get to third down. Be a better FIRST AND SECOND DOWN team."** And: *"When you're keeping them inside of six yards, that allows you to be more successful."*

**Against this defense, third-and-eight is a punt. That's the whole game plan in one sentence: win first down.**

## AND HE REFUSED TO CLAIM AN EDGE FROM HIS OWN HISTORY

This is the part I respect most, because he had an easy opportunity to sell himself and didn't take it.

Asked how much his understanding of what the 49ers want to do offensively helps him collaborate with Rob Leonard on the plan:

**"Yeah, you always have FAMILIARITY. But — it's the PLAYERS' GAME on Sunday. So we've got to get our players ready to do what THEY do best, so they can play at a high level on Sunday."**

**"It's the players' game on Sunday."**

Raider Nation, **that's a coach who spent a full season inside that organization refusing to pretend his inside knowledge wins a football game.** **Film doesn't tackle anybody.** And it echoes exactly what the film panel told us: *"Did you win your one-on-one matchup? Yes coach, or no coach."*

## NOW THE FAMILY STUFF — AND IT'S GENUINELY LOVELY

Raider Nation, this week is personal for Klint Kubiak, and the human material was wonderful.

**His family history with the Shanahans goes deep — his father coached with Mike Shanahan in Denver.** Asked for a first memory of watching their dads coach together:

**"Not a first one, though. Just really FORTUNATE to grow up in that environment. I didn't know it at the time, but I got to be EXPOSED TO A LOT OF FOOTBALL at a young age — and I'm very grateful for that, myself and my brothers."**

**And on what he learned from his brother, who he worked with for an entire year in San Francisco:**

**"I got to work with him side by side for an entire year. We were in college together, and then we went our separate ways and got married. And it was just SO GREAT reconnecting with him — seeing the type of HUSBAND he was, seeing how HARD he worked and how SMART he was. Things I already knew. But when you're kind of IN THE FIRE with your brother every day — it definitely builds a CLOSER BOND."**

**"In the fire with your brother every day."** Raider Nation, that's a beautiful way to describe a coaching season.

**And on that year with the 49ers: "It was a very IMPACTFUL season being around that organization. I could go on for a long time about how impactful it was — in all good ways."**

## AND THE FAMILY GROUP TEXT ANSWER MADE ME LAUGH OUT LOUD

Asked what the family group text looks like this week:

**"Well, my dad doesn't really know how to text very well. So — no, I think we kind of AVOID each other. See you, talk after the game."**

**"We kind of avoid each other. Talk after the game."**

Raider Nation, **that's every coaching family in America, and it's perfect.** Dad can't text. The brothers go quiet. **Everybody says what they have to say on Sunday afternoon and then they're a family again on Monday.**

## BUT HERE IS THE STORY THAT GOT ME — AND IT'S ABOUT US

Raider Nation, save this one. Somebody asked him what he remembers about coaching against the Raiders in Oakland, and about watching his dad go against them.

**And listen to what came out:**

**"I remember definitely, just overall with the Raiders — being in Denver, and EVERYBODY'S WALKING AROUND WITH RAIDER HATER SHIRTS ON. The town HAS A BUZZ TO IT."**

**"And I remember one specific game — I think it was in the SNOW. But Jerry Rice and TIM BROWN. And just, as a young kid, getting to WATCH ON THE SIDELINE. Those are your HEROES, you know."**

**"I just kind of remember the SNOW FALLING DOWN in the stadium, and watching those guys around the field. And TIM BROWN'S GOT HIS FINGER TAPE ROLLED UP — and then that week in MY game, I'M ROLLING UP MY FINGERS. Tim Brown doesn't need any gloves, neither do I."**

Raider Nation. **The head coach of the Las Vegas Raiders grew up in Denver taping his fingers like Tim Brown.**

**A kid on an opposing sideline, in the snow, watching Jerry Rice and Tim Brown wear the silver and black — and then going to his own game that week and copying it.** **No gloves. Finger tape rolled up. Because that's what Tim Brown did.**

**That man is our head coach.**

**And on the rivalry now: "It's a HUGE rivalry. I know both organizations for a long time have gone against each other. Times change — they're in Santa Clara and we're here in Vegas. And it's just a BLESSING to be a part of that rivalry moving forward."**

**A blessing. That's the word he used.**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON THE PLAYOFF HUNT IS EXACTLY RIGHT

A reporter framed it as a make-or-break stretch against really good teams. **And Kubiak refused the premise:**

**"I think EVERY WEEK'S that way. If you TREAT it that way, then you'll have success throughout the season. So we just keep your BLINDERS ON and focus on ONE OPPONENT AT A TIME."**

Raider Nation, **this team is NINTH in the AFC playoff picture** with Buffalo, New England, Kansas City, Denver and Jacksonville all in the mix. **The temptation to look at the standings is enormous.**

**Blinders on. One opponent.**

**And on the second-half starts — the thing we tracked all month: "Players EXECUTED. There's things you focus on every week, and sometimes you HIT them and sometimes you DON'T. But I thought our guys were very INTENTIONAL about that phase of the game. And obviously IT HAS TO CONTINUE."**

**Fourteen third-quarter points after three in four games. Intentional. And it has to continue.**

## SO WHAT ARE THE ACTUAL DECISIONS HERE?

Let me bring it home, Raider Nation.

**The "shocking" part isn't a lineup bombshell. It's two practice-field moves that tell you how this staff thinks.**

**One: they are stress-testing Jackson Powers-Johnson at a THIRD position** — left guard, after right guard and center — **because a man who can cover three spots is worth more than a late-round pick, and because his head coach thinks his energy elevates the whole offense.** **That's a man they're keeping.**

**Two: they are putting the number one overall pick behind the first-team line in warm-ups** — not because a change is coming, but because **"whether he gets three more years of reps or one more day of reps, he has to be ready."**

**Neither of those is a panic move. Both of them are a team that plans.**

**And the deadline is November 10th — four days away, right after this game.**
