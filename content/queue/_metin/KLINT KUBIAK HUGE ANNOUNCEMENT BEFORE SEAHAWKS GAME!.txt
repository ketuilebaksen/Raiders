**FERNANDO MENDOZA IS THE STARTING QUARTERBACK OF THE LAS VEGAS RAIDERS. SUNDAY. AT HOME. AGAINST THE DEFENDING WORLD CHAMPIONS.**

**And Kirk Cousins is a Minnesota Viking.**

Raider Nation, I want to do something honest with you today. **All week I was building the case for the other side.** I was going to sit here and argue that you ride Kirk Cousins into Seattle and give the kid his shot in a few weeks. **I had the whole thing mapped out.**

**And then the phone rang in John Spytek's office on deadline morning, and the argument became a museum piece in about six hours.**

**So today let's do both: let's walk through the case I was going to make — because it tells you EXACTLY what this rookie is walking into on Sunday — and then let's talk about Tom Brady putting a YEAR on a Raiders Super Bowl prediction.**

**Because Raider Nation, when Brady named that year, I immediately started counting seasons. And the number I landed on changes how you should feel about everything that happened this week.**

I'm Victor, this is Raiders Tunnel, we're pushing for **6,000 believers** — hit that like button, drop **"I AM A RAIDER"** in the comments, and let's get into it.

## FIRST: TOM BRADY PUT A YEAR ON IT

Raider Nation, this is the part I can't stop thinking about.

**Somebody asked Tom Brady — part owner of this football team — whether the Raiders would win a Super Bowl.**

**And I figured he'd give himself some wiggle room. I mean, come on. We're 4-5. Some days we can barely run the ball.**

**But there was A YEAR ATTACHED TO THAT QUESTION. And that's what got me.**

**Brady took a beat. And said TRUE.**

**And then — Raider Nation — they even gave him a chance to think about somebody playing that answer back in 2030. A reporter handed him the exit ramp.**

**HE WAS FINE WITH IT.**

**That part made me smile. Because look — when you own a piece of the Raiders, SURE, I expect you to believe in the team. That's the job.**

**Giving people A YEAR THEY CAN HOLD YOU TO? I did not expect that.**

## AND NOW COUNT THE SEASONS WITH ME, BECAUSE THIS IS THE WHOLE POINT

Raider Nation, do this math with me, because it's the most important math of the week.

**The Super Bowl after the 2029 season lands in 2030.**

**So when I picture the team Tom Brady is talking about, I'm picturing FERNANDO MENDOZA STILL ON HIS ROOKIE DEAL.**

**Raider Nation, that's where my mind went the second he said it.**

**Brady didn't answer a question about Sunday. He answered a question about a WINDOW. And that window is EXACTLY the length of a first-overall-pick quarterback's rookie contract.**

**Which means the clock on Tom Brady's prediction started ticking the moment John Spytek handed Fernando Mendoza the keys.**

**And here's what I was asking all week, before any of this happened: as a fan, I'd sure like to know HOW MUCH OF THAT TIME WE'RE WILLING TO SPEND WATCHING OUR FIRST OVERALL PICK STAND ON THE SIDELINE.**

**Well, Raider Nation — the answer turned out to be NINE GAMES. That's it. Nine games, and then the kid plays.**

## NOW THE CASE I WAS GOING TO MAKE — AND WHY IT STILL MATTERS

Raider Nation, stay with me here, because this isn't me being stubborn. **This is the actual scouting report on what Fernando Mendoza is walking into.**

**My argument was: stick with Cousins for Seattle, give the kid a chance in the next few weeks, and let him play while these games still MEAN something.**

**And the reason was this: you can watch practice all week. SUNDAY IS WHEN YOU FIND OUT WHAT THE KID TRUSTS WITH THE SAFETY CLOSING ON HIM.**

Raider Nation, **that sentence is still true. It's just that we find out this Sunday instead of in three weeks.** **And we find out against the defending champions.**

**And look — Klint Kubiak essentially agreed with me on the principle. He said it at the podium: "We all know that LIVE BULLET SUNDAY is FAR DIFFERENT from practice."**

**He knows. They all know. They did it anyway, because — in Spytek's words — Mendoza's practice level was "hard to ignore, IMPOSSIBLE to ignore."**

## AND I'M GOING TO GIVE KIRK COUSINS HIS DUE ON THE WAY OUT

Raider Nation, this matters, and I'm not going to skip it just because he's gone.

**I'll give Cousins his due: he SETTLED A COUPLE OF PLAYS DOWN when the spacing looked off.**

**And I'd MISSED THAT THE FIRST TIME — a rookie lets one of those mistakes turn into A WASTED POSSESSION.**

Raider Nation, **that's the invisible thing a veteran quarterback does and nobody claps for it.** **Routes get muddled, two receivers end up in the same window, the protection looks weird — and a twelve-year veteran just takes the air out of the play, finds the checkdown, and lives to play second down.**

**A rookie forces it. And then it's a punt. Or worse.**

**And of the three picks, only TWO THROWS actually bothered me.**

**The pick in the end zone right after the penalty on Michael Mayer bothered me MOST. We still had a chance to get some points out of that drive.**

**That last pick — the underthrow to Tre Tucker — was FOURTH AND ELEVEN with 3:57 LEFT and we were DOWN 38-17.** Raider Nation, **I LIKE the decision to take the shot there. Kubiak literally called it "a heave" and he was right.** **I just wanted Cousins to put it OUT IN FRONT of him.**

**And here's my final word on him: I think it's FAIR TO SAY WE'D HAVE HAD A HARD TIME GETTING TO FOUR WINS WITHOUT HIM.**

**Four wins. 19 touchdowns. Five interceptions before Sunday. A culture installed in a building that didn't have one in April.**

**Once a Raider, always a Raider. Mark Davis made sure that was the last word, and he was right to.**

## AND HERE'S THE SPECIFIC THING I WANT TO SEE FROM MENDOZA

Raider Nation, this is the test. Write it down, because it's the one I'm grading on Sunday.

**IT'S THE DEEP BALL TO TRE TUCKER.**

**You want Tucker RUNNING UNDERNEATH IT. When the ball gets there LATE or comes up SHORT, he has to WORK HIS WAY BACK — and the guy chasing him suddenly HAS A SHOT AT THE CATCH.**

**We've gotten away with that some weeks, because Tucker and Brock Bowers can hurt people on CROSSING ROUTES.**

**SAN FRANCISCO MADE US PAY.**

**So here's my question for the rookie: CAN HE GIVE TUCKER A BALL HE CAN CATCH WITHOUT EVER HAVING TO SLOW DOWN?**

Raider Nation, **that's the difference between a 60-yard touchdown and an interception, and it is maybe six inches of ball placement.** **Tucker's speed is only a weapon if the ball arrives where his speed is going.**

**And here's the honest version of the deal I'm making with this kid:**

**Say he sees that deep shot, makes the RIGHT READ, and MISSES TUCKER BY A STEP.**

**I CAN LEARN SOMETHING FROM THAT. Give him another shot later and see whether HE ADJUSTS.**

**That's the whole value of playing him now instead of in April. Raider Nation, if we wait until next season to let him work through ANY of that, we're going to be sitting here having the EXACT SAME CONVERSATION about how FEW GAMES HE'S PLAYED.**

**Right read, bad ball, then an adjustment — that's a successful day for a rookie. Remember that on Sunday when the first incompletion sails.**

## AND LET'S TALK ABOUT WHAT THE REPORTS SAID VERSUS WHAT HAPPENED

Raider Nation, I want to be transparent about something, because it's interesting and it's a lesson in how this league works.

**Over the weekend, Adam Schefter reported that NOBODY HAD CALLED THE RAIDERS about Cousins, and that Vegas had NO INTEREST IN DEALING HIM. The plan, per that report, was to START HIM UNTIL THE COACHES FELT MENDOZA WAS READY. Schefter also reported the Raiders had NO PLANS TO TRADE MAXX CROSBY.**

**And then on Tuesday, Kirk Cousins got traded to Minnesota.**

**Raider Nation, here's the thing — I don't think that report was wrong. I think it was TRUE WHEN IT WAS WRITTEN.**

**Because listen to what John Spytek said: "I NEVER MADE ONE CALL on Kirk Cousins. I got a few phone calls — but ONE REAL ONE THIS MORNING."**

**And: "All of a sudden you WAKE UP IN THE MORNING and you've got a decision to make, and you've got SIX HOURS to do it."**

**So both things are true. The Raiders weren't shopping him over the weekend. And then Minnesota called on Tuesday morning with something real, and six hours later it was done.**

**And the Crosby part of that report held up perfectly. Spytek: "We got a lot of calls on a lot of our players. WE SAY NO A LOT MORE THAN WE SAY YES."**

**MAXX CROSBY IS STILL A RAIDER. The report was right about that one.**

## NOW — WHAT DOES MENDOZA'S FIRST SERIES ACTUALLY LOOK LIKE? AND THIS IS THE SCARY PART

Raider Nation, this is where I need you to pay attention, because this is the real problem on Sunday and it has nothing to do with the quarterback.

**Ashton Jeanty and Mike Washington Jr. got SEVENTEEN TOUCHES between them and came away with SEVENTEEN YARDS.**

**And here's the breakdown: Jeanty ran SIX TIMES FOR 25 YARDS — then LOST FIVE YARDS ON FIVE CATCHES. Washington LOST THREE YARDS ON SIX CARRIES.**

**So when I say seventeen touches, that INCLUDES the times we threw them the ball.**

**Raider Nation, THINK ABOUT HOW MUCH YOU'RE ASKING OF A YOUNG QUARTERBACK when handing it off or flipping it out to a back gets you ABOUT A YARD.**

**That's the whole concern. Not Mendoza's arm. Not his reads. Not his nerve.**

**It's that a rookie quarterback's best friend in the world is second-and-five — and right now this offense keeps handing its quarterback second-and-ten.**

**And when you need somebody OUTSIDE to win a matchup on THIRD DOWN, Raider Nation — we're STILL LOOKING FOR THAT CLEAR NUMBER ONE RECEIVER.**

**That's the job description Fernando Mendoza just accepted: no reliable run game, no established number one receiver, and the defending champions in town.**

**That's why Kubiak kept saying "he's ONE-ELEVENTH of the operation." Because if the rookie thinks he has to be eleven-elevenths, we're in trouble.**

## AND THE PASS INTERFERENCE CALL — I'LL SAY IT, BUT I WON'T HIDE BEHIND IT

Raider Nation, I rewatched it.

**That pass interference call on Michael Mayer. I watched for the push and STILL DIDN'T SEE ENOUGH TO WIPE OUT A THREE-YARD TOUCHDOWN.**

**I DIDN'T LIKE THE FLAG. But I CAN'T PIN THAT LOSS ON THE REFS.**

**We turned it over five times. Nobody made us do that.**

**I bring it up because when you're getting SO LITTLE FROM YOUR BACKS, having a TOUCHDOWN CALLED BACK HURTS MORE than it would in a normal game. You don't have another way to get those three yards.**

**And honestly, Raider Nation, I APPRECIATED KLINT KUBIAK TAKING SOME OF THAT ON HIMSELF.** He said it Monday: **"It STARTS WITH ME — making sure we're OPENING RUN LANES for them and getting them in positions to TOUCH THE BALL MORE."**

**He's the guy calling the plays. So I'd expect him to ask whether he GAVE THE BACKS A FAIR SHOT.**

**And I'd ask the SAME of the guys BLOCKING for them.**
