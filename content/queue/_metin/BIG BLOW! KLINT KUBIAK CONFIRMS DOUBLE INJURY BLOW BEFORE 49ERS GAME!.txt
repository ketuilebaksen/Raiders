Raider Nation, and I want to be honest, because the easy version of this take is wrong.

**The part I'd worry about is what happens BEHIND that safety.**

**One step toward the handoff — ONE — and George Kittle can be running right through the space we just gave up.**

**Think about that.** You bring the safety down to stop McCaffrey. McCaffrey is the most dangerous back in football, so of course your linebackers' eyes go to him. **And Kittle releases into the exact window that safety just vacated.**

**And we KNOW how this works, because we did it to the Jets.**

**Klint Kubiak got Jaylen Nailor a two-yard touchdown from Cousins to put us ahead** — against a defense that knew what was coming. **That's what a good offensive mind does. He finds the soft spot and he gets his guy open even when you're expecting it.**

**Give Kyle Shanahan a soft spot in our zone and he'll do the exact same thing for Brock Purdy.**

**So here's my specific ask: I want our linebackers to make McCaffrey hesitate WITHOUT BOTH OF THEM stepping toward him on a fake. One guy still needs eyes on Kittle. Every single play.**

**That's the discipline test. Not talent. Discipline.**

**And if Shanahan does find Kittle underneath for six? FINE. Come back on second down and TACKLE SOMEBODY.**

**Raider Nation, the mistake — the real mistake — would be letting one completion scare us out of the very thing that gives us a chance to slow down Christian McCaffrey.** One Kittle catch does not invalidate the plan. **Panicking after one Kittle catch does.**

## THE RUN DEFENSE — AND WE HAVE TO START WITH WHAT THE JETS DID TO US

Raider Nation, I keep going back to the Jets tape, and not because of the win.

**Breece Hall ran for 147 yards on 21 carries.**

**One hundred and forty-seven. In a game we WON.**

**That's the whole disease in one sentence.** They doubled our interior, our linebackers got blocked before they could flow, **and the back picked a crease.**

**We had trouble with this exact thing against Kansas City. And New York watched that tape and knew EXACTLY where to go looking.**

**So the man I'm watching Sunday is Adam Butler. And Tonka Hemingway.**

**Can they HOLD THEIR GROUND long enough for a linebacker to get there CLEAN?**

**That's it. That's the whole question. Not "can they get a sack." Can they occupy a double team and keep an interior lineman OFF OUR LINEBACKERS.**

Raider Nation, we've said it on this channel for weeks and it is still the truest sentence about this football team: **keep the interior linemen off our linebackers and this defense is functional. Fail at that and nothing else matters.**

## BUT I ACTUALLY LIKE THE SCHEME MATCHUP THIS WEEK, AND HERE'S WHY

Raider Nation, here's the good news in the run-game breakdown.

**San Francisco likes to STRETCH the run toward the sideline.**

**I like that matchup a little better than what the Jets did to us.** Because a wide outside-zone run is a HORIZONTAL play — **and horizontal plays give Maxx Crosby a job he's elite at: SET THE EDGE.** **Make the back cut back inside. Don't let him turn the corner.**

**And when the run goes sideways, our linebackers get to RUN ACROSS THE FIELD instead of getting bulldozed in a phone booth.** **That's a much friendlier task than reading a double team at the point of attack.**

**But — and Raider Nation, this is the "but" — watch what happens if the edge defender gets TOO FAR UPFIELD.**

**McCaffrey only needs a LITTLE daylight to cut underneath him.**

**I want to see us turn him back toward help — and actually HAVE SOMEBODY WAITING THERE.**

**That second part was missing far too often against the Jets.** **We turned Hall back inside plenty of times. There was just nobody home when he got there.**

**Edge sets the wall. Linebacker fills the alley. That's the entire run defense on Sunday. Two jobs.**

**And they've picked up SIX OF EIGHT FOURTH DOWNS this year.** **So "third and short" isn't even a down for this team — it's a two-play sequence they expect to win.**

**Raider Nation, the way out of a five-game, thirty-point streak isn't a miracle. It starts with the stuff that looks ORDINARY on tape — like GETTING OFF A BLOCK before the runner reaches the sticks.**

**That's it. That's the whole fix. It's not sexy. It's not a trade. It's shedding a block on second and six.**

## NOW PUT YOURSELF IN KIRK COUSINS' SHOES — AND THIS IS THE DOUBLE BLOW

Raider Nation, here's where the injury report turns against us.

**Nick Bosa has been CLEARED TO PLAY.**

**DeForest Buckner has REJOINED that front.**

**Now, San Francisco has 15 sacks in seven games — and Raider Nation, that number does NOT tell me what the pocket is going to look like on Sunday.** Fifteen sacks in seven games is an ordinary number. **But that number was compiled largely WITHOUT Nick Bosa.**

**Bosa comes around the edge. Buckner pushes up the middle. And if Cousins steps FORWARD to avoid one of them, there has to be SOMEWHERE for him to put his feet and FINISH THE THROW.**

**That's the real problem with those two together.** It's not just the sack total. **It's that they take away the escape.** Bosa collapses the edge so you can't climb out. Buckner collapses the middle so you can't climb up. **And a 38-year-old quarterback who wins from a clean platform suddenly has no platform.**

**And remember what Cousins himself said about this front on Friday: "BOSA, BUCKNER — several of these guys are some of the BEST D-LINEMEN in the league."** **He knows. He said it out loud.**

## SO HERE IS HOW I'D PROTECT HIM

**One: GIVE DJ GLAZE HELP when he needs it.**

Raider Nation, I'm not asking a young right tackle to block Nick Bosa by himself for sixty minutes. **That's not coaching, that's hazing.**

**Let Michael Mayer CHIP on his way into a route.** Two-hand him on the way by, slow Bosa down a quarter of a second, then release. **Or bring in a SIXTH LINEMAN and just build a wall on that side.**

**Two: don't forget the OTHER side.**

**Keon White has the quickness to give Kolton Miller trouble.** **So this is not a one-man protection problem.** You can't slide everything to Glaze's side or you've just handed the other edge a free run at your quarterback.

**Three: know what's BEHIND the rush.**

**Fred Warner and Dre Greenlaw can get to the ball IN A HURRY.** **So the checkdown isn't free either.** There's no "just throw it to the back in the flat" against those two. **They close.**

**And we might even see an old friend at corner — Nate Hobbs was QUESTIONABLE on Friday, and I'd keep an eye on his status right up until kickoff.** Raider Nation, **imagine Nate Hobbs covering a Raiders receiver in the Bay Area. That's a storyline I did not have on my card this year.**
