# BREAKING! THE RAIDERS ARE RISKING EVERYTHING — "WE'RE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING" 🏴‍☠️

Raider Nation, I have to tell you exactly what this franchise just did, because it is the boldest decision of this entire season and it is going to divide this fan base right down the middle.

**They traded a quarterback who was leading the NFL in touchdown passes for a THIRD-ROUND PICK.**

**They handed the football to a 22-year-old rookie who has never played an NFL snap.**

**His first start is at home — against what Klint Kubiak himself just called THE BEST DEFENSE IN THE NFL.**

**And when a reporter asked whether they'd simplify the playbook to protect the kid, Kubiak said this:**

**"WE'RE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING."**

**Raider Nation, nothing held back. Full playbook. First NFL start. Defending world champions. 4-5 with eight games left and the playoff math still technically breathing.**

**THAT is risking everything. And I'm going to be honest with you — I love it.**

And then Fernando Mendoza walked up to that podium for his first press conference as the starting quarterback of the Las Vegas Raiders, **and what came out of his mouth was not what I expected from a 22-year-old.**

**He thanked Kirk Cousins by name. And Julie. And Cooper. And Turner.**

**He said the Cousins family "essentially took me into their household."**

**He said Kirk taught him everything "with NO RESTRICTIONS OR NO CONSTRAINTS."**

**And he said his motto is FAITH, FAMILY AND FOOTBALL.**

Raider Nation, strap in. I'm Victor, this is Raiders Tunnel, we're pushing for **6,000 believers** — hit that like button, drop **"I AM A RAIDER"** in the comments, and let's go through all of it.

## FIRST: HOW DID THE KID'S FIRST PRACTICE AS THE STARTER GO?

Raider Nation, Kubiak's answer was short and I think that's the point.

**"Went WELL. He was PREPARED."**

**And then a reporter asked the smart follow-up: you said yesterday he exceeded expectations in practice — is there a PARTICULAR MOMENT you recall that stood out the most?**

**And Kubiak's answer is one of the best things he's said all year: "There's NOT. And THAT'S A GOOD THING."**

**"It's CONSISTENCY. PLAYMAKING. KNOWLEDGE OF OUR SCHEME. His DAILY PREPARATION has been UP TO STANDARD."**

**"And now HE'S GOT TO GO PROVE IT IN THE GAME."**

Raider Nation, **I want you to understand how good that answer is.**

**He was handed a free opportunity to hype his rookie with a highlight story — the 60-yard bomb in camp, the scramble, the two-minute drill. Every coach in America takes that layup.**

**And Kubiak said: there ISN'T one moment, and that's EXACTLY what I want.**

**Because Raider Nation, a quarterback who gives you one spectacular Tuesday is a highlight. A quarterback who gives you the same Tuesday thirty weeks in a row is a STARTER.**

**No moment. Just consistency. That's the scouting report, and it's the right one.**

## AND HE WAS BLUNT ABOUT HOW HE HANDLED THE TRADE WITH THE TEAM

Raider Nation, this is the leadership part, and I respect how he did it.

**"Everybody's got a SMARTPHONE. EVERYONE KNOWS THE NEWS. We addressed what happened yesterday, and we MOVE ON. We wish Kirk the best, and we move on with FERNANDO and with AIDAN."**

**Asked what the message was to the guys about sending a locker room leader away:**

**"Yeah, that was it. Just THE HONESTY of it. Kirk WAS a leader. He IS one of the BEST QUARTERBACKS IN THE NFL. And he's A MEMBER OF OUR FAMILY HERE FOREVER."**

**And then — asked what this has been like for him as a FIRST-TIME HEAD COACH going through something like this:**

**"It's just BEING UPFRONT AND HONEST WITH THE TEAM. That's what I'D WANT IF I WAS A PLAYER. JUST TELL ME THE TRUTH. And let's MOVE ON TOGETHER."**

Raider Nation, **"just tell me the truth" is the entire Klint Kubiak operating manual in four words.**

**It's the thumb-pointing. It's "we all share the blame." It's "that was not those guys' job." It's "I want to stop short of saying he's earned it."**

**This man does not spin. Nine games in, that's established, and it's worth something.**

## AND HE WON'T LET ANYBODY CALL THIS A WHIRLWIND

Raider Nation, this was my favorite exchange of the day.

A reporter said everything's been a whirlwind for Fernando over the past 24 hours, and asked how he helps him mentally.

**Kubiak: "It SHOULDN'T BE A WHIRLWIND if he's been PREPARING THE RIGHT WAY EACH WEEK. At some point he KNEW he was going to get an opportunity — and so he's been preparing that way SINCE APRIL."**

**It shouldn't be a whirlwind.**

Raider Nation, **that's not a coach being cold. That's a coach refusing to hand his quarterback an excuse.**

**If you've prepared every week like the starter, then becoming the starter isn't chaos. It's Wednesday.**

**And then Mendoza got asked the same question at his own podium — do you RESONATE with that?**

**And the kid said: "I believe starting an NFL game, IT IS A NEW STEP. But a whirlwind? I would AGREE WITH COACH KUBIAK."**

**He agreed with his coach, and he still carved out the honest part: it IS a new step. Raider Nation, that's a 22-year-old threading a needle in his first press conference as a starter. That's poise.**

## NOW THE BIG ONE: THEY ARE NOT SIMPLIFYING THE OFFENSE

Raider Nation, this is the headline and it's going to be the thing fans argue about all week.

Asked whether going from a man who knew the offense for YEARS to a man in his FIRST YEAR changes the playbook or the VOLUME of the playbook:

**"We gathered information on what EVERY PLAYER CAN HANDLE going back to April. And we have an AWARENESS of his situation and being in his FIRST GAME."**

**"HOWEVER — WE'RE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING. We're going to go do WHAT THE WHOLE TEAM DOES BEST. ALL ELEVEN. And then HE DOES HIS PART: DISTRIBUTING THE FOOTBALL."**

**And he doubled down later, asked how he balances putting Fernando in comfortable situations while still running the full offense:**

**"I'm VERY COMFORTABLE running our playbook with Fernando. He's been through EVERY SITUATION that Kirk has been through in TRAINING CAMP and during OTAs. He just HASN'T HAD THE GAME REPS."**

**"But we're NOT going to ask him to do ANYTHING THAT WE HAVEN'T DONE IN PRACTICE."**

Raider Nation, **those last two sentences are the whole philosophy and they're smarter than "full playbook" sounds.**

**He's not saying "we'll throw everything at the kid and hope." He's saying: the playbook he's run since April IS the playbook. There's nothing in it that's new to him. Shrinking it wouldn't make him safer — it would just make the offense worse and tell the Seahawks exactly what's coming.**

**And Raider Nation, think about this from Seattle's side. If we come out in a watered-down, rookie-protection offense, Mike Macdonald eats that alive. He'll know we're not throwing past fifteen yards by the second drive.**

**Nothing held back is actually the SAFER call. It keeps them honest.**

**And Mendoza loved it. Asked about not being limited — "that has to excite you, right? Nothing held back on Sunday" — he went straight to the preparation:**

**"One of our mottos in the quarterback room is to ALWAYS STAY READY and ALWAYS STAY PREPARED. All three of us prepared LIKE THE STARTER. We were all in there doing our routine, doing it TOGETHER, watching film together, going over the game plan together, TWEAKING and seeing which ADJUSTMENTS we'd like to make."**

**Raider Nation, all three quarterbacks prepared like the starter, every week, together. THAT'S why there's no peeling back. The work is already done.**

## AND THEY WON'T PUT IT ALL ON HIM — AND THEY SAID IT FOUR TIMES

Raider Nation, count how many times this came up, because it's clearly a deliberate message.

Asked whether Fernando's MOBILITY and young ARM TALENT bring new possibilities:

**"He brings his OWN UNIQUE THINGS. And yes — he's a young man with SOME JUICE IN HIS LEGS, and he's got A LOT OF TALENT."**

**"But in this game on Sunday, it's going to be about WAY MORE than his PHYSICAL TALENT. It's going to be about his LEADERSHIP, his KNOWLEDGE OF THE OFFENSE, his ABILITY TO READ DEFENSE."**

**"And again, I'll say it — it's his RELIANCE ON HIS TEAMMATES and EVERYBODY AROUND HIM ELEVATING THEIR LEVEL OF PLAY. WE'RE NOT PUTTING IT ALL ON HIM."**

**And on Seattle: "Every week in the NFL is tough, and THIS IS THE BEST DEFENSE IN THE NFL. He's got to go DO HIS JOB and not worry about a whole lot else. EVERYBODY DOES THEIR ONE-ELEVENTH, and everyone around him ELEVATES THEIR PLAY."**

**And on leadership: "He just has to DO HIS JOB. You have to LEAD THROUGH PRODUCTION FIRST."**

Raider Nation, **"lead through production first" is such a healthy thing to say about a rookie.** **Nobody needs Fernando Mendoza giving a speech this week. Kirk Cousins gave the speeches. This kid needs to complete passes. The locker room will hand him the microphone when he's earned it, and not a minute before.**

**And the "one-eleventh" thing has now been said by the GM, the head coach, and the quarterback himself. That's a franchise speaking with one voice four days before a start.**

## AND NOW SEATTLE — THE BEST DEFENSE IN THE NFL, AND KUBIAK KNOWS IT INTIMATELY

Raider Nation, here's the twist that makes Sunday fascinating.

A reporter pointed out that **last year Kubiak had to go up against that Seattle defense EVERY WEEK IN PRACTICE** — and asked whether facing it is invaluable experience for a rookie.

**"IT IS. Yeah."**

**Raider Nation, think about that advantage.** **Our head coach spent an entire season scheming against that exact defense in practice every single day.** **He knows their calls. He knows their tendencies. He knows what Mike Macdonald does on third and seven when you motion a tight end.**

**That's the most valuable thing the Raiders have going for them on Sunday, and it has nothing to do with our roster.**

**And asked how his time in Seattle prepared him for this job, Kubiak lit up:**

**"I could answer that for a LONG TIME. Number one is just — ORGANIZATIONALLY, it's an ALL-FOOTBALL BUILDING. From JOHN SCHNEIDER to COACH MACDONALD — just extremely INTELLIGENT PEOPLE who TREAT PEOPLE THE RIGHT WAY and GET THE MOST OUT OF THEIR PEOPLE because they're GENERALLY GOOD PEOPLE."**

Raider Nation, **"an all-football building" and "they get the most out of people because they're good people" — that's the model he's trying to build HERE.** **The thumb-pointing, the "once a Raider always a Raider," the refusal to serve up a coordinator's head on a platter. He learned that somewhere, and he just told us where.**

**And then — the best moment of the day. Somebody asked if he got his RING.**

**"I DID. It's in the SAFETY DEPOSIT BOX."**

**Raider Nation, a Super Bowl ring, locked in a safety deposit box, while he coaches a 4-5 team with a rookie quarterback. He's not wearing it around the building. He's going to go get another one.**

**And now the team he won it with comes to our house.**

## AND HIS MESSAGE TO THE TEAM THIS WEEK IS EXACTLY RIGHT

Raider Nation, asked what he preaches most given his familiarity with the Seahawks:

**"The most important thing — like EVERY WEEK — is that you spend a lot of time WORRYING ABOUT YOU. Worrying about your TECHNIQUE, worrying about STUDYING, worrying about WATCHING TAPE."**

**"Yes, we've got to PREPARE THEM AS COACHES for what they're going to see. But as a player, you've got to make sure YOU DON'T OVERLOOK YOURSELF FIRST."**

**Don't overlook yourself first.**

Raider Nation, **that's the perfect message for a team that just gave up 38 points and set a franchise record for the wrong thing.** **You don't fix a 38-to-17 play count by studying Seattle harder. You fix it by shedding a block on second down.**

## AND THE OFFENSIVE LINE NEWS — THIS IS A REAL ANSWER

Raider Nation, here's actual roster news and it's good.

A reporter noted the depth chart lists **JPJ as the starting right guard** — same as last week — but **Trey got it.** Is that accurate? Is JPJ the starter?

**Kubiak: "Trey. TREY WILL BE THE STARTER — IF HE CAN GO. Yep."**

**TREY ZUHN III IS THE STARTING RIGHT GUARD IF HE'S HEALTHY.**

Raider Nation, **this is EXACTLY what I asked for.** **I went back through the San Francisco tape and that was the most comfortable I've felt watching our right guard all season — and I went in EXPECTING to find his mistakes and came out thinking I'd put them on the wrong guy.**

**Same guard, same tackle, same center, third straight week. Continuity. For a rookie quarterback. That's how you build a pocket.**

**And then somebody asked a terrific technical question — how INTERCHANGEABLE are the guards? Exclusively right and left, or interchangeable?**

**"They ARE INTERCHANGEABLE. Yeah. And A LOT OF THEM CAN PLAY CENTER AS WELL."**

Raider Nation, **that's quietly huge.** **An interior line where every man can slide one spot and several can snap the ball is a line that survives an injury mid-drive.** **Given that a starter went down with a concussion last week, that flexibility isn't theoretical — it's the thing keeping this offense upright.**

**And one more name worth noting: asked about Bryce Cable and what he needs to do to earn reps, Kubiak said "HE IS DOING IT, and he's doing a FANTASTIC JOB. Since the time he got here he's been a PROFESSIONAL. He's SHOWING IMPROVEMENT, and he's just WAITING FOR AN OPPORTUNITY."**

**Raider Nation, that's a coach telling a young lineman in public that the staff sees him. Those words get back to the locker room fast.**

## AND THE QUARTERBACK ROOM — AIDAN O'CONNELL DESERVES THIS

Raider Nation, this was the best question of the press conference and I'm glad somebody asked it.

**Aidan O'Connell has been in that room too, and his role changes with this move. What has Aidan meant to this development?**

**And Kubiak said: "That's a GREAT QUESTION and GREAT OBSERVANCE."**

**"Aidan is a PROFESSIONAL. He is an EXCELLENT PLAYER. He does things THE RIGHT WAY — VERY SIMILAR TO KIRK."**

**"To be in a quarterback room with those THREE GUYS STUDYING TOGETHER — THEY ALL MAKE EACH OTHER BETTER. You can't put enough value on having MATURE PLAYERS in there who are all working TOGETHER FOR THE COMMON GOOD."**

Raider Nation, **Aidan O'Connell just became the primary backup to a rookie making his first start against the best defense in football. He is one snap away. And he's spent nine weeks helping train the man who jumped him on the depth chart.**

**That's a pro. And notice Kubiak said "Fernando and AIDAN" twice when describing moving forward. He's not an afterthought in that building.**

## NOW FERNANDO MENDOZA'S FIRST PRESS CONFERENCE — AND HE OPENED WITH FAITH

Raider Nation, the first real question he got was about his faith — because he's referenced it at every big moment of his career, from the drive at Penn State to the national championship game.

**"This is a ONCE-IN-A-LIFETIME OPPORTUNITY, and I GET MY STRENGTH FROM JESUS CHRIST. I can't thank everybody that supported me up to this point, and a HUGE PART of that is FAITH."**

**"That's my motto: FAITH, FAMILY AND FOOTBALL. To have faith, and gain strength from my Lord and Savior Jesus Christ — it is a FUNDAMENTAL PART of my prior success."**

Raider Nation, **that's a young man telling you plainly where his steadiness comes from, and I think it's worth taking him at his word.** **He is about to start an NFL game at 22 years old against the defending champions, and he's got a foundation he's been standing on since college. Faith, family and football. In that order.**

## AND THEN HE SAID THE THING THAT TOLD ME EVERYTHING ABOUT HIM

Raider Nation, this is the part that got me, and I need you to hear it in full.

Asked how much Kirk helped put him in this position, Mendoza didn't answer the question — **he asked to make a STATEMENT first.**

**"I wanted to make a statement where I really, TRULY, FROM THE BOTTOM OF MY HEART wanted to thank KIRK and the ENTIRE COUSINS FAMILY — whether it was JULIE, COOPER, TURNER. They've as a FAMILY done so much for me."**

**"They ESSENTIALLY TOOK ME INTO THEIR HOUSEHOLD when they were here, and they WELCOMED ME WITH OPEN ARMS."**

**And then the honest part — the part that shows real emotional intelligence:**

**"It WOULD HAVE BEEN REALLY EASY for Kirk — being a VETERAN quarterback who's already PROVED EVERYTHING — to be A LITTLE BIT MORE DISMISSIVE."**

**"But he had me with OPEN ARMS and has taught me SO MANY THINGS — whether it's FILM STUDY, HOW TO OPERATE AS A STARTING QUARTERBACK, all the different MULTIFACETED APPROACHES that have made him so successful throughout his career."**

**"He has shared with WITH NO RESTRICTIONS OR NO CONSTRAINTS."**

**"So I really appreciate him. It was HEARTFELT. He was a GREAT MENTOR."**

Raider Nation. **A 22-year-old first overall pick, in his first press conference as the starting quarterback, named the WIFE and the CHILDREN of the man whose job he just took.**

**And "it would have been really easy for him to be a little bit more dismissive" is such a mature observation. He KNOWS what he was. He was the threat. He was the clock on the veteran's job. And the veteran taught him everything anyway, with no constraints.**

**Raider Nation, that's the real inheritance here. Not the third-round pick. That.**

**And Kubiak described the same thing from the outside: asked what Fernando gained from sitting behind Kirk —**

**"The number one thing would just be HOW TO PREPARE. How do I STRUCTURE MY WEEK? WHEN do I come in and get BODY WORK? WHEN am I getting a LIFT in? WHEN am I going to STUDY? WHAT TIME am I coming into the building? HOW LATE is Kirk staying? How am I WORKING WITH RECEIVERS IN BETWEEN PERIODS? What do I do POST-PRACTICE? WHAT QUESTIONS DO I NEED TO BE ASKING PROTECTION-WISE?"**

**"There's NOT ONE THING. You hope he generated a lot of KNOWLEDGE THROUGH OSMOSIS."**

**Raider Nation, knowledge through osmosis. Nine weeks of watching a twelve-year pro structure a week. That's the thing you cannot teach in a meeting room and cannot buy with a draft pick.**

## AND HE FOUND OUT ABOUT THE TRADE THE SAME WAY WE DID

Raider Nation, this is wild and I love it.

Asked if he had any inkling yesterday was going to unfold that way:

**"TRUTHFULLY, I HAD NO INKLING. I was just IN THE LOCKER ROOM yesterday, and PEOPLE ARE LIKE 'KIRK GOT TRADED.'"**

**"And everyone just said THAT'S THE NATURE OF THE BUSINESS, and that's how it is."**

**"And I'm EXCITED TO SEE KIRK IN HIS FUTURE."**

Raider Nation, **the first overall pick learned he was the starting quarterback of the Las Vegas Raiders because teammates told him in the locker room.**

**And Kubiak confirmed he wasn't going to discuss the private side of it: "I can't speak on that. But he KNEW, and WE KNEW, that at some point he was going to get an opportunity. So he's been preparing like that SINCE WEEK ONE."**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON PROCESSING IT WAS PURE QUARTERBACK ROOM

Raider Nation, asked how he processes the emotions and the responsibility:

**"I believe in the principles in the quarterback room that COACH JANOCKO, COACH KUBIAK and COACH SULLIVAN always preface: YOU'RE ALWAYS ONE PLAY AWAY. ALWAYS BE PREPARED."**

**"And KIRK WAS A GREAT EXAMPLE and leader of that — on how to be prepared, and HOW TO MAKE NO MOMENT TOO BIG."**

**"Those are all lessons that I believe AIDAN AND I will take into this next chapter."**

**HOW TO MAKE NO MOMENT TOO BIG.**

Raider Nation, **that's the sentence I'm going to be thinking about on Sunday when he takes his first snap and 60,000 people lose their minds.**

**And he brought Aidan with him again. Every single answer. "Aidan and I."**

## AND "SPRING FERNANDO" IS MY NEW FAVORITE THING

Raider Nation, this made me laugh out loud.

Asked how much he's grown since he arrived:

**"In the quarterback room sometimes, COACH SULLY will reference back to SPRING FERNANDO."**

**"I don't want to talk about myself in the THIRD PERSON — but it just shows how much IMPROVEMENT there's been."**

**"I'm a lot more CONFIDENT as a quarterback and I feel more DEVELOPED — whether it's UNDER CENTER, CONNECTING WITH TEAMMATES, UNDERSTANDING AN NFL SCHEME. There's so many areas I've improved on, and I look forward to KEEP ON IMPROVING, because THERE'S STILL A LOT OF WORK TO DO."**

Raider Nation, **"spring Fernando" is a coaching room joke that tells you something real: the staff has an internal benchmark for how bad he used to be, and they use it to show him how far he's come.**

**And notice the specific items: UNDER CENTER. Raider Nation, that's not a small one. A lot of college quarterbacks arrive having barely taken a snap with a hand under the center's backside. In a Kubiak offense — built on wide zone, play action and getting the quarterback's eyes down the field off a run fake — under center IS the offense. If he's gotten comfortable there, that's the single most important technical development of his rookie year.**

**And he closed with "there's still a lot of work to do." No chest-beating. On the day he was named the starter.**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON COMPETING WITH KIRK RATHER THAN AGAINST HIM

Raider Nation, one more and then I'll let you go.

**"That COMPETE-WITH approach was a FUNDAMENTAL PART of our relationship and the ENTIRE QUARTERBACK ROOM'S relationship."**

**"The quarterback room we had with Kirk was such a SPECIAL MOMENT. It was like a NICE FAMILY AWAY FROM HOME. We're all getting each other better — and honestly, it was MORE LIKE KIRK AND AIDAN GIVING ME TIPS AND TRICKS. So I'm FOREVER THANKFUL."**

**And then the line that ties the whole franchise together:**

**"At the end of the day, THE QUARTERBACK IS ONLY ONE-ELEVENTH. And I NEED TO DO MY JOB to BEST SHOWCASE ALL THE OTHER TALENTS we have on the offense and on the team."**

**And: "We have such a GREAT OFFENSIVE STAFF and such a GREAT OFFENSE — whether it's LEADERS ON THE OFFENSIVE LINE, LEADERS IN THE RECEIVER ROOM, LEADERS IN THE TIGHT END ROOM, LEADERS IN THE RUNNING BACK ROOM. I believe they're going to HELP ME ALONG THE WAY to best execute my one-eleventh so we can all move forward and be EFFICIENT AND EFFECTIVE as an offense."**

Raider Nation, **the general manager said one-eleventh. The head coach said one-eleventh. And now the rookie quarterback says one-eleventh, unprompted, twice.**

**That's not a talking point. That's a building that speaks the same language.**

## AND ONE LAST DETAIL THAT TELLS YOU HOW SERIOUS THIS WEEK IS

Raider Nation, asked what practice was like, Mendoza gave us a little window into the building:

**"Practice today was a WALKTHROUGH practice. I believe that's the FIRST TIME we've done a Wednesday walkthrough practice — or maybe ONCE BEFORE."**

**"So just, like Coach Kubiak says, WE NEED TO BE MATURE ON THESE WEDNESDAY PRACTICES — because although we're not competing with the defense, WE'RE COMPETING WITH OURSELVES."**

Raider Nation, **that's a schedule change.** **A Wednesday walkthrough instead of a full padded practice — with a new starting quarterback, in a week where they need every mental rep they can get and the legs can wait.**

**Teach, don't grind. And Kubiak's standard for it: be mature, compete with yourself. Which is exactly what he told the whole team: "don't overlook yourself first."**

**4-5. A rookie making his first NFL start. At home. Against the best defense in the NFL and the defending world champions. With a full, un-simplified playbook. With a run game that produced seventeen yards on seventeen touches last week. Without a clear number one receiver. And with an interior defensive line that has given up thirty points in six straight games.**

Because Trey Zuhn III is the right guard again this week and that line can get better while the quarterback is still cheap. Because Maxx Crosby is still a Raider. Because "we say no a lot more than we say yes."**

**And because a 22-year-old stood at a podium and thanked the wife and children of the man he replaced before anybody asked him a football question.**

**Raider Nation, I don't know if we win on Sunday. Seattle is the best defense in football and I'd be lying if I told you I was confident.**

**But I know the kid has been preparing like the starter since April. I know nothing's being held back. And I know the whole building is saying the same two words: one-eleventh.**
