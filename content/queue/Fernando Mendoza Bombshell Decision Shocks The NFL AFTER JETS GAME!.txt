Raider Nation, I sat through Klint Kubiak's entire Monday press conference after the **38-33** win over the Jets, and I want to tell you what the biggest Fernando Mendoza story of the week actually is.

**Nobody asked about him.**

**Not one question. Not a single reporter in that room brought up the first overall pick.**

Raider Nation, **think about where we were seven days ago.** Four straight losses. A 35-21 beating at home. Every podcast in America asking when the Raiders would turn the keys over. Jon Gruden telling teams to call about Kirk Cousins. **And Kubiak standing at a podium saying "eventually Fernando is going to play football. We have a plan. We don't have a timeline. It's not going to be this week."**

**One win later, the question has evaporated.**

**And it's not just silence — it's reporting.** **Ian Rapoport said on NFL GameDay Morning that the Raiders DO NOT WANT MENDOZA PLAYING ANYTIME SOON**, because the organization believes Cousins has performed well and **was never the cause of that losing streak.** The original plan was for the rookie to learn behind him, and **the team believes that process is working.**

**That's the bombshell decision: the Raiders have decided to be patient, and a 38-33 win just bought them the right to be.** Nobody traded anybody and nobody announced anything — **but a plan that was under siege eight days ago is now completely secure.**

And Monday's presser gave us a LOT more: **the Ashton Jeanty revelation that ends the whole controversy**, a rookie's first sack, a game-ending toe-tap in the back of the end zone, a kicker who beat the analytics, and the sentence about defensive backs that I think is the smartest thing this head coach has said all season.

## FIRST: WHAT KUBIAK ACTUALLY SAID ABOUT HIS QUARTERBACK

He was asked how it helps the rest of that offense to have a quarterback who's had his share of comebacks and is always calm and confident.

**And Kubiak went straight to the history: "He's got the GREATEST COMEBACK IN NFL HISTORY, right — with the Vikings and the Colts."**

Raider Nation, **that's a real thing.** Kirk Cousins owns the biggest comeback in the history of this league. **And on Sunday, down on the road with a four-game losing streak hanging around his neck, he produced four touchdowns and led another one.**

**"As an overall player, he's been a REAL BLESSING to our team. He's played really good football."**

**And then the part I respect most: "We're right around that halfway mark and we have a lot of football left to play. And NONE OF US ARE SATISFIED with where we're at. We just want to keep getting better."**

**Nineteen touchdown passes — the most in the National Football League. And "none of us are satisfied."**

**That's a 4-4 team refusing to celebrate .500. Good.**

## NOW THE REVELATION THAT ENDS THE JEANTY CONTROVERSY

Raider Nation, this is the single most important piece of information to come out of Monday, and I need you to hear it clearly.

**A reporter said: "Ashton said after the game that it was COMMUNICATED TO HIM that he was going to have fewer carries in this game."**

**He knew. In advance. They told him.**

**That kills the entire narrative.** This was not a benching. It was not a punishment for what he said about the Jets' tackling. It was not a loss of confidence. **It was a game plan that the player was informed of before kickoff, like a professional.**

**And Kubiak doubled down: "I stand by what I said. We're going to need Mike. We're going to need Ashton. We're going to need DYLAN. They're ALL part of our success, run and pass."**

**THREE backs. Washington, Jeanty, and Dylan Laube.**

**And then he said the thing nobody has given Jeanty credit for all season:**

**"Ashton made huge plays for us in the game yesterday in the pass game, IN PROTECTION. PHENOMENAL IN PROTECTION — like he's always been."**

Raider Nation, **phenomenal in protection.** Remember what Andrew Janocko told us weeks ago about running back pass protection? **"It's building callus. Understanding patterns of defenses and how their eyes work."** And the toughness: **"A want to physically put your face in there and take a bite — because your receiver is blocking for you downfield, so you've got to give him that extra tick to get open."**

**Nobody claps for that. It doesn't show up in a box score.** But Kirk Cousins threw for four touchdowns on Sunday, **and part of the reason he had time is that a 2025 top-ten pick was blowing up blitzing linebackers instead of carrying the football.**

**"Just the way the game went yesterday, Mike got more carries. Going forward, we're going to be counting on ALL THREE of those guys."**

**Rex Ryan called him "soft as char." His head coach called him phenomenal in protection. I know which one has watched the tape.**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON THE SHARED BACKFIELD IS THE BEST EXPLANATION OF THE NFL I'VE HEARD

A reporter brought up something Kubiak said back in OTAs — **that the great running backs don't want to come off the field** — and asked whether this is more of a shared backfield than he initially intended.

**And listen to this answer, because it explains this entire season:**

**"It's always a MOVING TARGET. You see where your team's at, and who's playing the best ball at the time. And it's a 17-GAME regular season where things change every week."**

**And then he listed it: "They've been changing at GUARD. They've changed up front DEFENSIVELY. Changed at CORNER. And that's the NFL."**

**"We have a YOUNG ROSTER, and we're going to give guys opportunities and see what fits us best."**

**And the line I want framed: "But you're ONE OF ELEVEN. Whether you're playing really well or not playing well at all — it's always going to change a little bit."**

Raider Nation, **"you're one of eleven."** That is the most honest thing a head coach can say to a star. **Nobody owns a job here.** Not the sixth overall pick, not the Rimington Trophy winner, not the veteran corner. **Guard, defensive line, corner, backfield — four position groups changed in eight weeks, and he's telling you that's normal, not panic.**

## AND WHY DID MIKE WASHINGTON EARN THOSE 25 CARRIES? THE ANSWER IS A COACHING CLINIC

Asked what Washington improved on to deserve that workload, Kubiak gave the clearest development philosophy of the season:

**"It goes back to PROVING IN PRACTICE. Having solid practice days. Being ON POINT with his ASSIGNMENTS. He's just continued to improve."**

**And then this, which is genuinely brilliant:**

**"Coming in as a young player, like all young players, there's a LOT to learn. And early on, there are things you DON'T WANT TO EXPOSE THEM TO. You want them to learn in the MEETING ROOM. You want them to learn the hard way at PRACTICE — so that you're NOT learning the hard way on SUNDAY."**

**"And everyone learns hard on Sunday — coaches and players. But it's been a DEVELOPMENT PROCESS to get him to this point."**

Raider Nation, **that's the answer to every fan who has screamed "just play the young guys" this season.** Including me, about Dont'e Thornton Jr.

**You protect a rookie from the moment he isn't ready for, because the price of that moment is a loss.** **Meeting room, then practice, then Sunday.** That's the ladder. **And Mike Washington Jr. climbed all three rungs before anybody handed him 25 carries.**

**And Kubiak refused to let it become a one-man story: "Outside of Mike — the OFFENSIVE LINE did a really good job. The TIGHT ENDS blocked well. Mike ran well. It gave us a spark in the run game, but I DON'T THINK IT WAS A ONE-MAN SHOW. It was a GROUP effort. And I think we were able to stay MORE BALANCED yesterday."**

**More balanced. That's the word he's been chasing for a month.**

## AND NOW TREY ZUHN — AND THE MOST HONEST THING HE SAID ALL DAY

Asked about the rookie's first career start at right guard:

**"I thought Trey was CALM in there. I thought he was WELL PREPARED. That was NOT A SURPRISE — but I thought he played well. There are things he's going to want to improve on. But I was pleased, along with the coaching staff, with the EFFORT he put on the field and what his FUTURE looks like for us."**

**"That was not a surprise."** Raider Nation, remember what he told us about why they could shuffle this line in Week 8: **Rick Dennison and that offensive line staff cross-trained these men in TRAINING CAMP** — **"there are a lot of reps that we BANKED, that we're using now to our advantage."**

**Calm and well prepared in his first NFL start, and the head coach says he expected it. That's the bank account paying out.**

**And then came the question every Raiders fan wanted: did he perform well enough to keep getting opportunities?**

**"I believe so. Yes."**

**But then the follow-up about whether he'd actually keep PLAYING — and Kubiak gave the most ruthless, most honest answer of the entire press conference:**

**"I think he has to STEAL ANOTHER GUY'S JOB. That's what you've got to do. You've got to LEAPFROG SOMEBODY."**

Raider Nation, **that is the same sentence he used about Dont'e Thornton Jr. three weeks ago** — *"He's got to take the spot of another guy. He's got to go beat out somebody."* **And it's the same philosophy as "you can't sit in those chairs if you're not an accountable human."**

**Nothing is given in that building. A rookie can start, play well, get publicly praised — and still have to steal the job to keep it.**

**And remember what Kubiak said about the men he replaced:** that **Caleb Rogers and JPJ** are **"great teammates"** who **"really supported Trey, and that speaks to their character."** **Those two are going to try to steal it right back. That's the whole point.**

## TIM KEENAN III GOT HIS FIRST NFL SACK — AND HE EARNED IT THE SAME WAY

Here's a story I love, and it's the same lesson wearing a different number.

**Rookie Tim Keenan III got his first career sack on Sunday**, after being elevated over the last couple of weeks.

**And Kubiak explained exactly why: "Another guy that's showed up IN PRACTICE. A guy that has really good PRACTICE HABITS. His overall ATTITUDE — he's got WINNING ATTRIBUTES. He's a good teammate. He's got good energy. He's playing hard. He's EARNING his opportunities."**

**"Winning attributes."**

Raider Nation, **connect this to the thing that stung last week.** **Jonah Laulu — a Las Vegas kid who started 15 games and had four sacks a year ago — was a healthy scratch while Tim Keenan III and JJ Pegues dressed ahead of him.** And then six teams claimed Laulu off waivers, and Tennessee got him.

**I questioned that decision. I said the league disagreed with our evaluation.**

**And then the rookie they kept instead got a sack in a win.**

**I'm not declaring the Laulu decision correct — one sack doesn't settle it.** But Raider Nation, **this is what "practice habits" buys you in this building, and I owe this staff an acknowledgment: the young man they chose produced.**

## NOW THE SECONDARY — AND THE INJURY NEWS

**Darien Porter, who left after ONE PLAY: "It's a groin. We'll continue to monitor throughout the week. We'll just kind of see how he progresses."**

**A groin injury, week-to-week. That's the update.**

**And a young corner went in immediately after Porter went down.** Asked why he was first up, Kubiak said: **"We wanted to get him some EXPOSURE. And that's going to continue."**

Raider Nation, **"we wanted to get him some exposure" is a sentence that tells you how this staff thinks about the back half of a season.** They aren't just surviving. **They're using real snaps to find out what they have.**

**And on that rookie plus Zeke: "Zeke made some plays for us. Had a big TIPPED PASS."** And then the honesty: **"In his first action, he's going to continue to get better. He's got plenty of things to improve on — as does Zeke, as does DP, as does EVERYBODY. But we feel like we're going to continue to ROTATE those guys through."**

**Rotation is now the plan at corner. That's officially an open competition.**

## AND THE ERIC STOKES ANSWER IS THE SMARTEST THING HE'S SAID ALL YEAR

Raider Nation, this is the part I want you to really sit with.

A reporter asked whether there was any consideration of pulling Eric Stokes, who struggled at times.

**"No. Stokes ALSO made some really big plays for us. He had a TOUGH ASSIGNMENT. They made some plays on him — but he made some really big plays for us, especially IN THE RED ZONE. He's been a great competitor for us."**

**And he's right.** Stokes made **two important third-down stops that limited New York to field goals — including on a possession that reached first-and-goal at the Raiders' 5-yard line.** **That's a huge sequence in a five-point win.**

**But then Kubiak added the competitive edge: "As the competition continues and young guys make plays — WE'RE GOING TO PLAY THE BEST GUYS."**

**And then came the masterpiece.** Asked whether it surprised him that Stokes was targeted so much:

**"I think the Jets had a really good plan overall. And when you play defensive back in the NFL — you're GOING to get beat. At some point you're going to get beat. But HOW DO YOU RESPOND when that happens?"**

**"When a defensive back gets beat, EVERYBODY KNOWS ABOUT IT. If a GUARD loses in the run game — it's NOT DISCUSSED AS MUCH. So you've got to have THICK SKIN. You've got to be able to move on to the next down."**

**"And I'm really glad we have NUMBER FIVE."**

Raider Nation, **that is the fairest thing I've heard a coach say about the hardest position in football.**

**Every single one of us — me included — has spent this season yelling about corners getting beat.** And he's right: **a guard can lose a one-on-one on every single run play and nobody says his name.** A corner loses once and it's on every highlight show in America. **It's the only position in football where failure is televised in isolation.**

**"How do you respond" is the only question that matters. And he publicly backed his guy while telling him the competition is real. That's how you coach a man.**

## JACK BECH PLAYED — AND HE BROUGHT SOMETHING THE BOX SCORE WON'T SHOW

**Jack Bech returned to the field on Sunday** after a **broken forearm.**

**Kubiak: "The FRESH LEGS showed up. Jack was the SAME PLAYER he was when he left. He kept himself in really good condition, put himself in position to play some meaningful snaps for us."**

**And then the value: "Jack brings a TOUGHNESS to our team that has EXTREME VALUE — and that showed up IN THE RUN GAME yesterday."**

Raider Nation, **I told you this was coming.** Last week I explained the hidden thing Bech does — **the Jakobi Meyers point: you watch the running back turn the corner, and then you see on the replay who actually kept that corner open.**

**And on a day when Mike Washington carried it 25 times and this offense "stayed more balanced," Kubiak specifically said Bech's toughness showed up IN THE RUN GAME.**

**A receiver who blocks is how four-yard runs become eight-yard runs. That's the whole thing.**

## AND JALEN NAILOR GOT HIS FIRST TOUCHDOWN CATCH

Asked how rewarding it was to see Nailor score after everything he's been through this season:

**"He's EARNED it in practice, too. Jalen's put together some really good practices. And I'm NOT SURPRISED by the game that he had. He's deserving of MORE opportunities — and he made the most of his yesterday."**

Raider Nation, **give Kubiak his credit here.** On Friday, when the entire country was saying this offense couldn't separate against man coverage, **he pushed back specifically about Nailor: "Jalen IS getting separation. He IS getting open down the field. It's time on task."**

**Then Nailor caught two passes on the opening touchdown drive and scored his first touchdown of the year.**

**"I'm not surprised." He told us two days early.**

## NOW THE PLAY THAT ENDED THE FOOTBALL GAME: JEREMY CHINN'S TOE TAP

Raider Nation, I saved my favorite for here.

**Jeremy Chinn ended this game with an interception in the BACK OF THE END ZONE — a toe tap to keep his feet in bounds.**

And they asked Kubiak — **who played safety himself** — what his reaction was:

**"It was IMPRESSIVE. That was a BIG TIME catch. When the ball's in the air, you're just looking up there and PRAYING one of our guys comes down with it. And Chinn was extremely AGGRESSIVE. He wanted to END THE GAME RIGHT THERE — and got his feet in bounds. TREMENDOUS athletic play."**

**"He wanted to end the game right there."**

Raider Nation, **think about what that interception actually means to this defense.** This group has forced **ONE takeaway in four straight losses** after getting ten in the first three games. We have talked about that drought every single week.

**And it ended with a safety toe-tapping in the back of the end zone to win a football game.**

**That's not just a play. That's a drought breaking at the exact moment it needed to.**

## AND THE DEFENSE'S SECOND HALF WAS A TRANSFORMATION

Raider Nation, here's a number from Monday that I did not have yesterday, and it's remarkable.

**THE JETS RAN THE BALL ONLY FOUR TIMES IN THE SECOND HALF.**

**Four carries. In an entire half.** After running for **140 yards in the first half** against a defense ranked 30th in the league.

Asked if the Raiders did something specific to prevent it, Kubiak protected his own scheme — correctly: **"We definitely had discussions at halftime. We made adjustments. I can't comment on them, what they did."**

**But: "I do know that the RESILIENCY of our team showed up — of our defense showed up — and we FINISHED the game."**

And on the first half struggle, he started by giving the opponent credit, which I always respect: **"Number one, I'd CREDIT THE JETS. They did a great job of moving the ball on us in the run game."**

**And then the realism: "That's something, as a coaching staff and as players, that we're working together to fix. And it's NOT GOING TO HAPPEN OVERNIGHT. It's going to take practices. It's going to take EXPERIENCE. It's going to take us putting our heads together and having a better product, so we're not giving up that many yards on the ground."**

**"Not going to happen overnight." Raider Nation, that's the honest answer about a run defense allowing 146 yards a game — one of the worst in football.**

**But then he told us what he believes in: "The people we have doing it together — our players and coaches — I BELIEVE IN. And we have EACH OTHER'S BACK."**

**"And the thing I LOVE about our defense is that when things DIDN'T go well — we KEPT PLAYING. We kept playing. We got key stops. Made a huge play at the end with Chinn."**

**"In the NFL, it's not always going to be pretty. It's not always going to be how you wanted it to happen. But at the end of the game, it's WHO WON THE GAME. And I think as a team, we FOUND A WAY TO WIN."**

## AND HE WASN'T SATISFIED WITH HOW IT ENDED — EVEN IN A WIN

This is the part that tells you he's not grading on the scoreboard.

**"Offensively, I would have LOVED to finish that four-minute drive with the ball and get another first down. I was pleased with the couple of first downs we did get — but we've got to finish that game ON A KNEE."**

**"On a knee."** Raider Nation, that's the standard: **a four-minute drive that ends with the quarterback kneeling, not with a punt and a prayer.**

**"But when we didn't, MATT stepped up and made a CLUTCH KICK, and the defense got the stop."**

## AND HERE'S THE DECISION THAT WENT AGAINST THE ANALYTICS

Now this is interesting, and nobody is talking about it.

**A reporter pointed out that the analytics FAVORED PUNTING in that situation — and Kubiak sent his kicker out instead.**

**His reasoning: "I just see him kick in practice. I've seen the CLUTCH KICKS that he's made. We were at the line that we had CONFIDENCE in, on that yard line. We've got to get the ball closer for him offensively — but we had confidence in Matt."**

Raider Nation, **that's a head coach overruling a spreadsheet because of what he watches every single day.** And he was right. **The kick went through, and this team is 4-4 instead of 3-5.**

**And notice he still took responsibility for the field position: "We've got to get the ball closer for him offensively."** **Even the successful decision came with a note about his own unit's failure.** Six straight weeks of this.

## AND THE INJURY THAT WORRIES ME MOST

**Tonka Hemingway played only 13 SNAPS.**

**Kubiak: "He had a STINGER come up in the game, and we continue to evaluate him."**

Raider Nation, **this is the one I'm watching all week.** **Hemingway is the snap leader on that interior defensive line and has started five games this season.** He just cleared the concussion protocol two weeks ago. **And now a stinger limited him to 13 snaps in a game where the Jets ran for 140 in a half.**

**Those two facts are connected.** The whole diagnosis of this defense — *"our interior guys need to do a better job of beating their one-on-one blocks and keeping those interior guys off our linebackers"* — **gets a lot harder when your best interior lineman plays 13 snaps.**

## AND HIS ANSWER ON BOWERS AND MAYER TELLS YOU WHAT HE VALUES MOST

Asked what it is about their skill sets that makes them such a good fit, Kubiak **did not start with football**:

**"Number one is being A+ TEAMMATES. Very UNSELFISH individuals — that play in the RUN GAME and the PASS GAME. And that PERMEATES throughout the whole offense and the whole team."**

**"When you have your best players, your most unselfish players, and your greatest teammates — THAT'S the attribute that is MOST IMPORTANT TO ME."**

Raider Nation, **that's the culture statement of this regime, and it explains every single roster decision we've argued about for two months.**

**Why Dylan Laube is a two-time captain. Why Isaiah Pola-Mao stayed a leader after losing his job. Why Caleb Rogers and JPJ helped Trey Zuhn get ready to take their snaps. Why Jonah Laulu's spot went to a rookie with better practice habits. Why Jamari Thrash lasted three weeks.**

**Your best players also have to be your best teammates. That's the filter.**

## AND ON ENDING THE STREAK: "GO 1-0 EVERY WEEK"

Asked how important it was to snap a four-game skid and turn the page:

**"It was important. It was important to go ONE AND OH every week. And that's the CHARACTER of our locker room. We had a four-game losing streak, and you've got to find a way to STOP THE BLEEDING."**

**"It was NOT EASY yesterday. We were down, we came back, we finished strong. It was a GREAT TEAM VICTORY."**

**"Stop the bleeding." They did.**

## SO WHERE DOES THE MENDOZA QUESTION ACTUALLY STAND?

Let me bring it home, Raider Nation, because this is the real answer to the title.

**The Mendoza decision has been made, and it's this: he waits.**

**Rapoport reported the organization doesn't want him playing anytime soon.** Kubiak called Cousins **"a real blessing"** and pointed to **the greatest comeback in NFL history.** **Cousins leads the league with 19 touchdown passes.** And Kubiak's own framing from last week stands: **"Eventually Fernando is going to play football. We have a plan. We don't have a timeline. But at the end of the day, it is going to be MY decision."**

**And the most telling evidence of all: in a full Monday press conference after a win, not one reporter in that room asked about him.**

**That's what winning does. It buys a plan the time it needs.**

**If Cousins falters, the conversation returns immediately — and it should.** **But the first overall pick is doing exactly what the staff intended: learning in the meeting room, so he doesn't have to learn the hard way on a Sunday.**

**And now everything runs through San Francisco.** **The trade deadline falls AFTER that game.** Win and you're **5-4**, you're a buyer, Maxx Crosby stays, and Spytek should be dialing Indianapolis about an interior defensive lineman that same afternoon. **Lose and you're 4-5 with the phone ringing.**
