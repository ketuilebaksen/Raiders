## FIRST: WHAT HAPPENED IN SAN FRANCISCO

Raider Nation, let's start with the game that set this whole conversation on fire.

**Last Sunday in San Francisco, Ashton Jeanty touched the ball ELEVEN TIMES and gained TWENTY YARDS.**

**SIX CARRIES FOR 25 YARDS. And FIVE CATCHES FOR A LOSS OF FIVE YARDS.**

**And the week before that, in New York, HE HAD TWO CARRIES. Two carries for nine yards — while a FOURTH-ROUND ROOKIE named MIKE WASHINGTON JR. ran the ball TWENTY-FIVE TIMES.**

**And the reason for that was A COACH'S CALL. Klint Kubiak said so himself.**

**Raider Nation, Ashton Jeanty was the SIXTH OVERALL PICK in the NFL draft. And the Raiders are now averaging the FEWEST RUSHING YARDS IN THE LEAGUE since they took him.**

**So a lot of people are asking a lot of different questions, and they deserve real answers.**

## THE DECLINE IS REAL, AND HERE IT IS IN BLACK AND WHITE

Raider Nation, let's look at the split, because it's stark.

**IN THE FIRST FOUR GAMES of this season: 264 RUSHING YARDS. SIXTY-SIX A GAME.**

**But 102 OF THEM CAME AGAINST MIAMI IN WEEK ONE.**

**IN THE FIVE GAMES SINCE: NINETY-EIGHT YARDS. Nineteen point six a game.**

**That works out to UNDER THREE YARDS A CARRY.**

**And nobody else is carrying the load either. In that same five-game stretch, the team's second back, Mike Washington Jr., kept getting handed the ball.**

**On the season: JEANTY HAS 362 RUSHING YARDS. WASHINGTON HAS 275.**

**Raider Nation, THE FOURTH-ROUND ROOKIE IS WITHIN EIGHTY-SEVEN YARDS OF THE SIXTH OVERALL PICK.**

**And if you zoom out — SINCE THE RAIDERS DRAFTED HIM, THIS TEAM AVERAGES SEVENTY-FIVE RUSHING YARDS A GAME. That is the LOWEST IN THE LEAGUE.**

**And 3.31 YARDS A CARRY — also the LOWEST IN THE LEAGUE.**

**Raider Nation, much like so many of the defensive numbers on this team, we are LAST. And that's not a narrative. That's arithmetic.**

## AND THE EXPECTATIONS MAKE IT WORSE

Raider Nation, to understand why this hurts so much, you have to remember what he was SUPPOSED to be.

**Jeanty was the SIXTH OVERALL PICK in the 2025 NFL draft. THE FIRST RUNNING BACK OFF THE BOARD. And the HIGHEST-DRAFTED RUNNING BACK SINCE SAQUON BARKLEY WENT SECOND IN 2018.**

**And Bleacher Report wrote last October that THE RAIDERS SHOULD HAVE TAKEN TACKLE ARMAND MEMBOU INSTEAD — because quality running backs are always found later in the draft.**

**Raider Nation, sit with that for a second. The criticism at the time was: don't spend the sixth pick on a running back, spend it on an OFFENSIVE LINEMAN.**

**And here we are in Week 10, and the thing everybody agrees is broken is... the OFFENSIVE LINE.**

**That's a brutal piece of foresight and it deserves to be acknowledged.**

**And his rookie year did not prove them wrong: HE RAN FOR 975 YARDS — the EIGHTEENTH MOST out of SIXTY PLAYERS with 500 yards across 2025 and 2026.**

**He had TWO standout games — 138 YARDS AT CHICAGO in Week 4 and 128 AGAINST HOUSTON in Week 16 — and ALMOST NOTHING AROUND THOSE TWO GAMES.**

**And the advanced numbers were worse: he had the LOWEST RUSHING EPA IN THE NFL as a rookie. His rushing DVOA was MINUS 4.7.**

**AND THIS SEASON IT IS MINUS 28.3 PERCENT.**

**Raider Nation, that's not a dip. That's a collapse.**

**And remember: the Raiders SAID IN MAY that they intended to use him as their WORKHORSE.**

**A workhorse back with SIX CARRIES is not a workhorse. Period.**

## AND THE EFFICIENCY NUMBERS ARE GENUINELY UGLY

Raider Nation, I'm not going to protect him from these. Here they are.

**NEXT GEN STATS has him STUFFED ON TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT OF HIS CARRIES — the HIGHEST RATE among running backs with at least fifty carries.**

**One in four carries goes NOWHERE. The worst in football.**

**And the analysis from Fantasy Points found that FORTY-SEVEN PERCENT OF HIS CAREER RUNS HAVE BEEN HIT AT OR BEHIND THE LINE OF SCRIMMAGE — across 180 CARRIES.**

**Forty-seven percent. Nearly HALF of every run he's ever taken in the NFL, somebody has put hands on him before he reached the line.**

**And Rex Ryan — two weeks ago — called Ashton Jeanty THE WORST DRAFT PICK OF THE LAST TWENTY YEARS AS A RUNNING BACK.**

**And fans are noticing other things too. On one clip of his six carries against San Francisco, people are pointing out that HIS BODY LANGUAGE JUST LOOKS HORRIBLE.**

Raider Nation, **I'm going to be careful here, because body language from a still frame is the cheapest evidence in sports. But I won't pretend nobody's saying it. If you're a sixth overall pick getting six carries and five targets that lose five yards, I don't know how you look thrilled about it either.**

**And I don't know if it's one hundred percent his fault. But a chunk of it does fall back on him.**

## BUT NOW — RAIDER NATION, HERE COMES THE NUMBER THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING

Raider Nation, stop everything. This is the statistic that reframes this entire debate, and I need you to understand it.

**THIS SEASON, ASHTON JEANTY GETS ONE YARD BEFORE CONTACT PER CARRY. DEAD LAST AMONGST ALL RUNNING BACKS.**

**TWENTY-ONE BACKS AVERAGE MORE THAN TWO.**

**One yard. Before anybody touches him. The worst in the National Football League.**

**Raider Nation, "yards before contact" is the cleanest measurement in football of what your OFFENSIVE LINE gave you. It is not a running back stat. It's a BLOCKING stat. It measures how much free space existed before a defender arrived.**

**And ours is the lowest in the league. By a mile.**

**And here's the piece of evidence that absolutely nails it:**

**HE HAS HAD MORE THAN TWO YARDS BEFORE CONTACT IN ONLY TWO OF HIS TWENTY-FIVE CAREER GAMES.**

**AND IN THOSE TWO GAMES, HE AVERAGED 5.86 YARDS A CARRY — THE SECOND BEST AMONG ALL RUNNING BACKS.**

**RAIDER NATION. READ THAT AGAIN.**

**Twice in his career — TWICE in twenty-five games — this young man has gotten ordinary NFL blocking.**

**AND BOTH TIMES HE WAS THE SECOND-BEST RUNNING BACK IN FOOTBALL.**

**5.86 yards a carry. That's not "serviceable." That's ELITE.**

**So the honest conclusion is this: a running back who is hit at the line on almost HALF of his runs IS NOT GETTING THAT MUCH HELP. And when he HAS gotten help, he's been devastating.**

**Raider Nation, that's not a bust. That's a Ferrari with no road.**

## AND THERE'S ONE MORE NUMBER THAT SEALS IT

Raider Nation, this one is the clincher and almost nobody has brought it up.

**As of early October, the data had Washington's rushing value at PLUS 1.1 PERCENT and Jeanty at MINUS 28 PERCENT.**

**Which looks damning for Jeanty — until you see this:**

**JEANTY WAS GETTING 2.63 YARDS AFTER CONTACT PER CARRY at that same point.**

**Raider Nation, do you understand what that means?**

**ONE yard BEFORE contact. And 2.63 yards AFTER contact.**

**He is generating MORE THAN TWO AND A HALF TIMES more yardage AFTER a defender hits him than before one does.**

**That means the run game DOESN'T EVEN HAVE A CHANCE TO SUCCEED BEFORE IT GETS GOING. The play is already broken when he receives the ball, and he's dragging it to three and a half yards with his legs.**

**2.63 yards after contact is a genuinely good number. In a vacuum, that's a tough, physical, competitive back.**

**He's just running into a wall on first contact at the line of scrimmage, every single snap.**

## AND NOW LET'S TRACE EXACTLY WHEN HE LOST THE BACKFIELD

Raider Nation, this is the timeline, and it starts on a specific day.

**OCTOBER 18TH, AGAINST BUFFALO. That's where it shifted.**

**Jeanty had TEN CARRIES FOR 22 YARDS. Washington had NINE CARRIES FOR 43.**

**And after that game, it was said that Klint Kubiak WANTED PRODUCTION OUT OF ALL THREE OF HIS BACKS.**

**A WEEK LATER, AGAINST THE RAMS: Jeanty averaged 1.7 YARDS A CARRY and the Raiders had 37 YARDS ON 14 PLAYS.**

**AND THEN CAME THE JETS GAME ON NOVEMBER 1ST.**

**Jeanty had NO TOUCH IN THE FIRST QUARTER. His first one came with SIX MINUTES LEFT IN THE FIRST HALF.**

**He finished with TWO CARRIES FOR NINE YARDS — and a FORTY-FIVE YARD CATCH.**

**And Washington had TWENTY-FIVE CARRIES FOR EIGHTY-THREE.**

**And Kubiak said the two carries WERE HIS CALL. And that Jeanty is "STILL GOING TO BE A HUGE PART OF THE RUN GAME MOVING FORWARD" and "a BIG PART OF THIS TEAM MOVING FORWARD."**

**And Jeanty himself said THE PLAN WAS COMMUNICATED TO HIM BEFORE THE START OF THAT GAME.**

Raider Nation, **that last detail matters and I want to give it its due. He wasn't ambushed. They told him before kickoff. He didn't complain. That's a professional.**

## AND YES — WE HAVE TO TALK ABOUT THE BULLETIN BOARD THING

Raider Nation, there's context to that Jets game and we're not going to pretend there isn't.

**Before that game, during the week, Jeanty said a couple of the Jets defenders DID NOT REALLY WANT TO TACKLE — basically calling out their team.**

**Some of their players called it BULLETIN BOARD MATERIAL. And some people were speculating that maybe the Raiders were trying to SEND ASHTON JEANTY A MESSAGE for doing stuff like that.**

**However — Klint Kubiak spoke out on it directly and said NO. That had NOTHING to do with it and DID NOT FACTOR into their decision.**

Raider Nation, **I'm going to take the head coach at his word there, and here's why: he had every opportunity to let that narrative live. A coach who wanted to discipline a young player quietly would have loved the ambiguity.**

**He killed it instead. That's consistent with everything else this man does — remember "that was NOT THOSE GUYS' JOB" about the coverage bust, and refusing to name the struggling veterans.**

## AND THEN SAN FRANCISCO GOT GENUINELY STRANGE

Raider Nation, this is where the usage stops making sense to me.

**Over the last three games, Jeanty has SIXTEEN CARRIES and Washington has THIRTY-SEVEN — though twenty-five of those came in one game.**

**But in San Francisco, look at this:**

**JEANTY PLAYED ABOUT SIXTY-FOUR PERCENT OF THE SNAPS. Washington played THIRTY-FOUR PERCENT.**

**AND THEY BOTH HAD SIX CARRIES.**

**Jeanty had TWENTY-TWO ROUTES RUN and was TARGETED SEVEN TIMES.**

**Washington ran THREE ROUTES and got NO TARGETS.**

**And those seven targets turned into THREE CATCHES FOR A LOSS OF FIVE YARDS.**

Raider Nation, **stop and look at what that actually describes.**

**The sixth overall pick — a 215-pound downhill power runner — played nearly two-thirds of the snaps and spent TWENTY-TWO of them RUNNING PASS ROUTES.**

**Twenty-two routes and six carries. For a WORKHORSE BACK.**

**Raider Nation, that's not a running back's job description. That's a slot receiver's.**

**And I explained last week exactly why those targets died: WHEN FRED WARNER CAN TAKE HIS FIRST STEP DOWNHILL, A SWING PASS MIGHT AS WELL TELL HIM WHERE TO MEET THE RUNNER.**

**Negative five yards on five catches isn't a bad running back. That's a play design meeting the best linebacker in football with nothing in his way.**

**And Washington wasn't the answer either: he finished that game with A LOSS OF THREE YARDS ON SIX TOUCHES.**

**Raider Nation, BOTH backs went backwards. When both of your backs lose yardage, the problem is not the backs.**

## AND KUBIAK'S TWO ANSWERS STILL DON'T RECONCILE

Raider Nation, here's the tension.

**On November 4th, asked whether Jeanty can still be a BELLCOW: "YES. ABSOLUTELY. That's EXACTLY how I envision him on this team."**

**He said Jeanty is "A YOUNG PLAYER WITH A REALLY BRIGHT FUTURE" and that he's "SO GLAD THE RAIDERS HAVE HIM."**

**And on THAT SAME DAY, he said the SPLIT between Jeanty and Washington will be EVALUATED ON A WEEK-TO-WEEK BASIS.**

**And the offensive coordinator said RUNNING THE BALL IS A FULL TEAM GAME — that it doesn't fall on one player specifically.**

Raider Nation, **"he's my bellcow, absolutely" and "we'll evaluate the split weekly" are two different football philosophies. A bellcow doesn't have a weekly split. That's what the word MEANS.**

**But here's the generous read, and I think it's the true one: he's protecting a 21-year-old's confidence in public while telling the truth about the plan.**

**And honestly? This week he said it even more plainly than before. Asked what he can do DIFFERENTLY to fix the run game:**

**"It's a FAIR QUESTION. I DEFINITELY HAVE SEEN STRIDES. I know IT DIDN'T SHOW UP LAST WEEK. And there's THINGS THAT I CAN DO BETTER. There's THINGS OUR PLAYERS CAN DO BETTER."**

**And asked whether the offense's success depends on Jeanty running effectively: "TEAM SUCCESS WILL COME WHEN THAT AREA IMPROVES — ON OFFENSE AND ON DEFENSE."**

**Raider Nation, that's four straight press conferences where he's put the run game on himself FIRST. On Monday: "it STARTS WITH ME — making sure we're OPENING RUN LANES for them."**

## AND NOW THE ACTUAL ANNOUNCEMENT: A NEW RIGHT GUARD

Raider Nation, this is the part of the story that matters most, because it's an ACTION instead of a quote.

**If the offensive line is half the problem, the Raiders have tried to make attempts to fix it.**

**KUBIAK MOVED CALEB ROGERS INTO THE RIGHT GUARD SPOT IN PLACE OF JPJ.**

**And he said FIXING THE RUN GAME FALLS ON HIM, and they're CONTINUING TO WORK AT IT AND TWEAK THINGS.**

Raider Nation, **that is the third different configuration at right guard in about three weeks.** **JPJ was listed there. Trey Zuhn III actually played it against San Francisco and was — by my own film review — the most comfortable I'd felt watching that spot all season. Then Zuhn went down with a concussion. Kubiak said this week "TREY WILL BE THE STARTER IF HE CAN GO." And now Caleb Rogers has moved in.**

**And remember what Kubiak told us about this group: "They ARE INTERCHANGEABLE. And A LOT OF THEM CAN PLAY CENTER AS WELL."**

**So here's my honest read: that interchangeability is a strength when a man goes down mid-game. It's a WEAKNESS when it means nobody ever plays twenty straight snaps next to the same two people.**

**Raider Nation, a run game is built on SEMI-TELEPATHY between a guard and a tackle. You cannot develop that with a new combination every Sunday.**

**And some of the numbers suggest THE PROBLEM ACTUALLY PREDATES a lot of these moves. The one yard before contact. The 47 percent hit at or behind the line. Twenty-five percent stuffed. All of that was true BEFORE anybody got shuffled.**

## AND THE FAN REACTION HAS GOTTEN GENUINELY DARK

Raider Nation, let me show you where the public is on this, because it's worse than you think.

**His dynasty ADP is at an ALL-TIME LOW — 18.3, per the DLF data.**

**And people are using the phrase GENUINE MALPRACTICE about a game where he got five attempts and three targets.**

**And that list of all-time-low values is a GRAVEYARD OF EXPECTATIONS — Breece Hall is at 45.3. Saquon Barkley's on there. All of them at their own lowest points.**

**AND JEANTY IS THE NAME ON TOP OF IT ALL.**

**And it's been pointed out that HIS DROPS CONTINUE TO BE AN ISSUE — and that the ball is going to keep being taken away from him if that continues.**

**And people are saying it's TIME TO HIT THE PANIC BUTTON. And that THE BACKFIELD NO LONGER BELONGS TO ASHTON JEANTY like it maybe once did.**

Raider Nation, **the drops are the one criticism I can't wave away with an offensive line argument. Seven targets, three catches is a 43 percent catch rate. That's on him. Kubiak called the quarterback "the best decision maker on the team" as a standard — well, "catch the ball" is the running back's version of that standard.**

**If you want more touches, catch the ones you get. That's fair and it's true.**

## AND THE CLOCK — HERE'S WHY THIS SEASON MATTERS SO MUCH

Raider Nation, here's the business reality.

**HIS ROOKIE DEAL RUNS THROUGH 2028, WITH 2029 BEING A CLUB OPTION.**

**So the Raiders have TWO MORE YEARS OF CONTROL to find out what he is — before they have to make a massive decision.**

**Raider Nation, that's actually good news and here's why: there is no financial urgency. Nobody has to decide anything about Ashton Jeanty today. He's cheap, he's 21, he's under contract.**

**Which means the only reason to panic is EMOTIONAL. And emotional decisions about 21-year-olds are how franchises give away good players.**

**And remember — this is the same front office that said "WE SAY NO A LOT MORE THAN WE SAY YES" when teams called about their players at the deadline.**

## AND THE NEXT THREE GAMES DECIDE EVERYTHING

Raider Nation, here's the stretch: **SEATTLE. Then DENVER. Then CLEVELAND.**

**That's a stretch Kubiak has to think about and game plan for. And it's a stretch where the USAGE and UTILIZATION of these players is really going to get called into question.**

**And here's the cleanest framework I can give you for Sunday — this is the test:**

**IF THE OFFENSIVE LINE IS MORE SUPPORTIVE AND JEANTY GETS FIFTEEN CARRIES AND THE LINE GIVES HIM SOMETHING — THEN WE LEARN SOMETHING ABOUT JEANTY, and what these numbers are actually telling us.**

**BUT IF HE GETS SIX CARRIES AND SEVEN TARGETS AGAIN — THEN WE LEARN SOMETHING ABOUT KUBIAK, and what the utilization is going to look like going forward.**

**Raider Nation, that's it. That's the whole thing. Fifteen carries and we find out about the player. Six carries and twenty-two routes and we find out about the plan.**

## AND HERE'S MY HONEST ANSWER

Raider Nation, I've waited until now, and here it is.

**I AM NOT READY TO CALL HIM A BUST. NOT CLOSE.**

**But I DO think there needs to be a change in the way they're utilizing him right now. And I DO think HE NEEDS HELP.**

**And look — much like a LOT of guys on this team need help. They're still a YOUNG TEAM. They're DEFINITELY FAR FROM PERFECT. And it goes right back to the defense too, where changes genuinely need to be made.**

**Raider Nation, here's the summary of every number in this video in two sentences:**

**ONE YARD BEFORE CONTACT — WORST IN THE LEAGUE. 2.63 YARDS AFTER CONTACT — GENUINELY GOOD. HIT AT OR BEHIND THE LINE ON 47% OF HIS CAREER RUNS. AND IN THE ONLY TWO GAMES OF HIS CAREER WHERE HE GOT NORMAL BLOCKING, HE AVERAGED 5.86 A CARRY — SECOND BEST IN FOOTBALL.**

**That's not a bad running back. That's a good running back behind a bad run-blocking operation, being used as a slot receiver on swing passes that the best linebackers in football eat for breakfast.**

**And yes — he has to catch the ball better. Both things are true.**

## AND HERE'S WHAT MAKES SUNDAY FASCINATING

Raider Nation, it's going to be very interesting — **because they're NOT CHANGING THE PLAYBOOK.**

**Kubiak said it: "WE ARE NOT PEELING BACK ANYTHING. We're going to do WHAT THE WHOLE TEAM DOES BEST, ALL ELEVEN, and then HE DOES HIS PART: DISTRIBUTING THE FOOTBALL."**

**Mendoza is just coming in. Same offense. New quarterback. First game without Kirk Cousins.**

**And a lot of people are speculating that THE BACKFIELD LOOKS WIDE OPEN now that Fernando Mendoza has taken over.**

**Raider Nation, here's why I actually think this is the week Jeanty's usage goes UP, not down:**

**A ROOKIE QUARTERBACK NEEDS A RUN GAME MORE THAN A VETERAN DOES.** **Kirk Cousins could survive second-and-ten. A 22-year-old making his first start against the best defense in the NFL cannot. Second-and-five is where rookies learn. Second-and-ten is where they get hurt and where interceptions get thrown.**

**And Seattle allows 89 rushing yards a game and held Bijan Robinson to 63 on 20 carries. So this is simultaneously the hardest possible week to run it and the week we most need to.**
