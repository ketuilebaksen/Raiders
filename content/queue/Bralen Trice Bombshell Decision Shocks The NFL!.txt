## FIRST, THE NEW NEWS: TENNESSEE TOOK JONAH LAULU

Let's start with the development nobody had yesterday.

**On Tuesday, the Raiders waived Jonah Laulu to make room for Dont'e Thornton Jr.'s activation. And the Tennessee Titans have since claimed him.**

**Good for Jonah.** Raider Nation, I mean that sincerely. **This is a LAS VEGAS KID. A hometown guy who started 15 games last season and recorded four sacks.** This year he played in five games and finished with **five tackles**, and he was a **healthy scratch against the Rams** after **rookie Tim Keenan III and JJ Pegues passed him on the depth chart.**

**And Raiders Wire said it bluntly: the plan to bench him so the defense could bulk up against the run FAILED MISERABLY.**

**But here's why the Titans claim matters so much — and this is the part I want you to sit with.**

**A 26-year-old who started 15 games and got four sacks last season was available on waivers for roughly 48 hours, and another NFL team grabbed him immediately.**

**That means the league did not agree with our evaluation.**

Raider Nation, I'm not going to call it a disaster — **he'd fallen to fourth or fifth in that rotation and his role had genuinely shrunk.** But you cannot waive a productive hometown starter, watch a rival claim him within days, and pretend the market gave you a thumbs up. **It didn't. Somebody else saw value we decided we didn't need.**

**And remember: this defense is 30th in the NFL against the run.**

## NOW THE MAN THEY TOOK INSTEAD — AND HIS STORY IS SOMETHING ELSE

**On Wednesday the Raiders signed Bralen Trice to the practice squad.** Listed as a defensive end. **A big man, around 6'4" and 270 pounds.**

**Atlanta took him in the third round of the 2024 draft, out of Washington — 74TH OVERALL.** And for about five minutes, that looked like a perfect marriage.

**Then the football gods showed up with a pair of scissors.**

**He tore his ACL in his very first preseason game — so his rookie season was gone before it began.** He rehabbed, he worked his way back, **and then he aggravated the SAME KNEE heading into 2025 and lost that entire season too.**

**Two years. Two years of rehab, two years of watching from the sideline, and a whole lot of time to stare at a ceiling.**

## AND HERE IS THE PART THAT MAKES THIS A BOMBSHELL

Raider Nation, look at what the Falcons actually did.

**They spent a premium pick on him. They waited patiently through two lost seasons. He finally got healthy, he finally got back on the field, he finally appeared in games.**

**And then they let him go.**

**In the span of a few days in October, Atlanta put him on the active roster — and then waived him.**

**That's not a slow fade. That's a team promoting a man to the 53 and changing its mind before the ink dried.** And I'll be honest — **I find that genuinely strange.** You don't burn a third-round pick and two years of patience and then make a decision that fast. Something in that building decided, very suddenly, that they'd seen enough.

**And that's exactly how he landed in Las Vegas.**

## SO YOU CAN READ THIS ONE OF TWO WAYS

**Way number one:** it's just a practice squad signing. Nothing to see here, folks, move along.

**Way number two:** a former third-round pick with real upside and an ugly injury history just landed in the lap of a defense that is BEGGING for help.

## AND THE TAPE IS GENUINELY NOT BAD

Here's why I actually like this move, and why Raiders fans online are at least willing to give it a minute.

**Trice finally played this season, and honestly? It wasn't bad.** The breakdown making the rounds has him at **76 defensive snaps over four games in 2026, with FIVE PRESSURES, a quarterback hit, a pass breakup, and two tackles.**

**That is not Pro Bowl production and I'm not going to insult your intelligence by pretending it is.** Two tackles is two tackles.

**But remember who we're talking about. This is a man two years removed from a torn ACL, getting his first real NFL snaps against professional offensive tackles.**

**And the number a lot of us keep circling back to: an 18% PASS RUSH WIN RATE in that stretch.**

Raider Nation, **for a player in his situation, winning nearly one of every five reps is a number worth noticing.**

**The tape notes are encouraging too.** The description is **good initial burst off the line, lower body power, and the length to get depth on a tackle.** There was a clip where the quarterback escaped his grasp — **but the scouting notes specifically liked his CHASE-DOWN EFFORT.**

**And that matters more than people realize.** You do not see a man hustling after a quarterback like that **two years after a devastating knee injury** unless he's carrying some serious motivation.

**And go back to college: the reports called him a physical EDGE SETTER who can win with POWER. At Washington he piled up 16 SACKS and 23.5 TACKLES FOR LOSS over his sophomore and junior seasons, and earned All-Pac-12 honors.**

**That is not a nobody. That is a man who PRODUCED before the injuries stole his time.**

## SO LET'S CALL IT WHAT IT IS

**One analyst called this signing a "flyer." And that's exactly right. It's a flyer. A lottery ticket.**

**But on a team that cannot stop anybody right now, a lottery ticket from the right pile can be a very big deal.**

**The Falcons gave him a look and moved on. The Raiders — in the middle of what everyone politely calls a rebuild — looked at the same man and said "we'll take a swing."**

**If you're the kind of person who says break the glass in case of emergency, Raider Nation: Las Vegas just broke it.**

## NOW LET'S BE HONEST ABOUT HOW BAD IT IS ON DEFENSE

Because this signing only makes sense against the backdrop.

**The Raiders are 3-4 and have lost four in a row. And in every one of those four games, they have allowed 30 OR MORE POINTS.**

**Thirty. Forty-five. Thirty-three. Thirty-five.**

**Four straight games of 30-plus TIES the longest such stretch by this franchise since 1999 — and the last time it happened was 2020.**

**And the defensive line has been the heartbeat of the problem.** They aren't consistently getting to the quarterback, **and they are getting bullied against the run.**

**And Sunday: Los Angeles ran for 199. The Raiders ran for 49. Down 28-6 at halftime. One-for-five in the red zone.**

**That's not a football game. That's a hostage situation.**

## BUT THE OFFENSE IS NOT THE MAIN CULPRIT, AND I WANT THAT ON THE RECORD

**Through Week 6, the Raiders were putting up around 27 POINTS A GAME — SIXTH BEST IN THE LEAGUE.**

**Sixth. That is a genuinely respectable number** and it's the single most under-discussed fact about this season.

**But the ground game has been a ghost town.** Before the Rams game, this team had been **held under 75 rushing yards in FOUR STRAIGHT GAMES.** **And then they went out and ran for 49 more.**

Raider Nation, **that's five games.** Five games of a running game that doesn't exist, on a team whose head coach just said **"quit throwing it so much."** You can't run it if you're never ahead, and you're never ahead because the defense gives up 36.

## NOW THE OTHER TWO MOVES, BECAUSE THEY STACK UP DIFFERENTLY

**On Tuesday the Raiders activated receiver Dont'e Thornton Jr. from injured reserve** — and that's the one everybody has been arguing about.

**He was a fourth-round pick in 2025 out of Tennessee with a reported 4.30 forty.** And at Tennessee in 2024, **he averaged 25.4 YARDS A CATCH, which led the ENTIRE FBS, with six catches of 50 yards or more.**

**Twenty-five point four. League-leading. Six catches of fifty-plus.**

**He caught 10 passes for 135 yards as a rookie, then hurt his hamstring in training camp and has missed all seven games this season.**

**Kubiak said last week that Thornton was "making big progress" and had "done a lot of really good things in practice."** And **today we got another update: Thornton AND Jack Bech are both back at practice.** **Bech was designated to return from injured reserve on Monday after a BROKEN FOREARM** — though remember, **he still has to be activated before he can play**, so I'm not putting him in Sunday's lineup yet.

**One fan was guessing Thornton could see 15 to 20 snaps against the Jets with some deep targets. And honestly? That is his game.** **That's a guess, not an announcement** — but if you're finally dressing a 4.30 receiver who led college football in yards per catch, **you do it by throwing it over somebody's head.**

**For context on why this matters: Tre Tucker leads all Raiders receivers with 29 catches for 474 yards and four touchdowns.** That's the whole top of the depth chart. **There is plenty of room for somebody to be a spark.**

**This team needs something to click. And now they've got two more pieces back in the building.**

## AND IT HAPPENS TO BE THE STRETCH WHERE ONE FAMOUS MAN FINDS OUT WHERE HE PLAYS NEXT

Which brings me to **Maxx Crosby**, because with the deadline 13 days away, **the rumors keep circling him like vultures over the desert.**

**Let's recap.** **In March, the Raiders AGREED to trade Crosby to the Ravens for TWO FIRST-ROUND PICKS. Then it fell apart when he failed his physical on a meniscus problem.** So the Raiders ended up with the same star they already had, **and the deal evaporated like a puddle on the Vegas Strip.**

**He's signed through 2029. And through seven games this year, he has TWO SACKS.**

**Two.** For a man whose name is usually synonymous with chaos, **that is a quiet year.** And I'm not hiding from it. **But you know the context: the highest rate of chips and double teams of his entire career — sixteen of them in the Buffalo game alone — on a defense ranked 30th against the run.** When nobody wins inside, **the edge gets erased.**

**And here's what's really important: Crosby has insisted ALL YEAR that he wants to stay.** He said it again this week — **the team is not playing good enough right now, and he wants to remain a Raider.**

**Let's also not pretend there's no market.** **The Bears, the Cowboys, the Patriots and the 49ers have all been linked to pass rushers like him since the summer.**

**But ESPN's Adam Schefter said on the Pat McAfee Show on Monday that the trade talk surrounding Crosby has LOST A LITTLE BIT OF STEAM.** And he added the line that should end this debate:

**If you're the Raiders, you do NOT want to sell off the heart of your team and the face of your franchise.**

## HERE IS MY TAKE, AND IT'S ABOUT MORE THAN ONE PLAYER

Raider Nation, step back and look at what this week actually was, because I think there's a real philosophy hiding in it.

**In 48 hours this front office took a swing on a 25-year-old with two rebuilt knees and a 74th-overall pedigree, cut loose a productive hometown player who'd fallen out of the rotation, activated a 4.30 receiver who'd never played a snap this year, and got another second-round pick back onto the practice field.**

**That's not a rebuild by blockbuster. That's a rebuild by VOLUME OF SWINGS.** Lots of cheap lottery tickets, fast decisions, and no sentimentality. **Spytek is playing a numbers game at the bottom of the roster while keeping every single draft pick he owns except a 2027 seventh.**

**And I mostly like it.** **But the Titans claiming Jonah Laulu within days is the cost of playing that way** — sometimes you're wrong, and the league tells you immediately, in public.

**So here's my verdict: keep Crosby, keep swinging at the margins, and for the love of everything, fix the run defense from the inside.** Because **no practice squad flyer and no deadline trade saves you from 199 rushing yards and 36 points a game.** That's assignments, alignment and tackling — **and as Nico Dean said, it's eleven men deciding to stop being mercenaries.**
