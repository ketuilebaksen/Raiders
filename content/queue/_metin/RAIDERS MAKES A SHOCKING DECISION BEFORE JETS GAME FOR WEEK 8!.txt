## FIRST: WHAT THE LAULU DECISION ACTUALLY COST

Let me be fair to both sides, because that's how we do this.

**The case for the move:** Laulu had been **inactive against Kansas City AND the Rams.** He wasn't playing. He'd fallen behind **Folorunso Fatukasi, Tonka Hemingway and Thomas Booker IV**, with **JJ Pegues and rookie Tim Keenan III** dressing ahead of him. **A roster spot is only worth what the man occupying it produces, and he was producing nothing because he wasn't on the field.**

**The case against:** With the way this defense has struggled to stop the run, **I would have liked to keep that depth.** Five tackles in five games is not the résumé of a man you build around — **but 15 starts and four sacks a year ago is not nothing either**, and six teams agreed.

**And the question the Schultz report raises: could we have gotten a late pick for him?**

**Honestly? Probably not, and here's why — claiming a guy off waivers is a lot easier than giving up a pick for him.** If six teams can simply raise a hand and take him for free, **nobody is calling you to offer a seventh-rounder.** The waiver system punishes you for cutting a man other teams want. **That's the lesson, and it's a real one: if you believe six teams will claim him, you trade him BEFORE you waive him.**

**And the uncomfortable timing: the Jets, who we play Sunday, wanted our run-defense depth.** They didn't get him. **But they looked at our roster and saw something worth having, which tells you how they're scouting us.**

**And here's the detail about his rookie year that I did not have until today, and it gives me pause:**

**Eight starts, Raider Nation. Ten catches.**

**Because that's the real test.** A 4.30 forty and **25.4 yards a catch leading the FBS** means nothing if the corner is sitting ten yards deep waiting for the bomb. **If he dresses Sunday, I give him that opportunity on our first couple of drives.** Find out early, while the game is even.

## AND I'D FEEL BETTER ABOUT ALL OF IT IF I KNEW WHO WAS BLOCKING

Now the right guard situation, and it's genuinely unsettled.

**Jackson Powers-Johnson and Caleb Rogers have been sharing time at right guard.** **And JPJ missed Thursday with a BACK INJURY and was questionable on Friday.** **Trey Zuhn III could get a look there too, with Spencer Burford on the left.**

**For a back trying to trust his cut, that matters enormously.**

**And here's the technical reason why.** On a power run, **you want the guard and tackle moving the first defender TOGETHER — with one of them coming off that block for the linebacker.** Two men displace the first body, then one peels off to meet the second.

**Leave that linebacker free, and he's waiting in the hole.**

Raider Nation, **that is the single most common reason a run dies at the line of scrimmage**, and it's a communication failure, not a talent failure. **Two men have to agree, in half a second, on who comes off.** And you cannot build that agreement when the man beside you changes every week.

**Which is exactly the tension this staff is living in.** Kubiak said he'd **"keep shaking it up until we find the right mix."** **He's right that the mix is wrong. He's also creating new communication problems every time he shuffles.** There's no clean answer. **But I'd rather find the five men now than in December.**

## NOW THE DEFENSIVE PLAN — AND THIS IS WHERE WE CAN WIN THE GAME

Raider Nation, here's the matchup that gives me genuine hope.

**Geno Smith has thrown TWO interceptions through seven games.** So I'm not expecting gifts. **And I'm certainly not counting on him to throw us one just because he used to play here.**

**But he has been SACKED 21 TIMES.**

**Twenty-one sacks in seven games. THERE is our opportunity.**

**And here's how I'd attack it: I'd like our front to get there with FOUR, so we can keep help behind the corner covering Wilson.**

**That's the chess match in one sentence.** If you rush four and get home, you keep a safety over the top of their best receiver. **If you have to send five or six, that help disappears and Wilson gets a one-on-one.**

**Because if Geno has TIME, he can simply wait for his receiver to clear that help.** A quarterback with a clean pocket beats bracket coverage every time — he just holds it until the route wins.

**So: make him move BEFORE he wants to.** Flush him off his spot, break the timing, **and now a defensive back has a chance to break on the throw.**

Raider Nation, **that's exactly what Rob Leonard was asking out loud two weeks ago: "Can we rush with four and get home?"** Sunday is the day we find out. **And it's why Maxx Crosby seeing sixteen chips and double teams in one game matters so much** — if the interior can't win, four won't be enough.
