If you didn't watch that game in London, here's the short version. **The Jacksonville Jaguars lost to the Houston Texans 34-31.** And **Brian Thomas Jr.** — the 24-year-old who made the Pro Bowl as a rookie — **played 15 snaps, caught nothing on two targets, dropped a catchable ball on the opening drive, and did not see the field for the entire second half.**

**Travis Hunter took over a bigger role on offense and posted 59 yards and a touchdown.**

**And through six games, Thomas has nine catches for 105 yards and one touchdown.**

Raider Nation, for a player who was supposed to be a star in this league, **those are brutal numbers.** And **ESPN's Dan Graziano has written that Thomas has fallen to third in Jacksonville's receiver pecking order — behind Parker Washington and Jakobi Meyers.**

**Third. On his own depth chart. A year after a Pro Bowl.**

## DEFOREST BUCKNER AND THE CROSBY PROBLEM

Now the defensive tackle conversation — and this is where I get genuinely excited.

**Through six games, DeForest Buckner has 26 tackles and four sacks.** That is **exactly** the kind of interior production this Raiders defense does not have.

And here's the stat that explains why it matters so much. **Maxx Crosby is seeing the highest rate of chips and double teams of his entire career — sixteen of them in the Buffalo game alone.**

**Sixteen.** In one game.

Raider Nation, **if there's nobody on the inside who scares an offense, Maxx Crosby will be doubled until the end of time.** That's not a Crosby problem. **That's a help problem.** You can be the best edge rusher in football and still be erased by a chip from a tight end and a slide from a guard — and that's what's happening to him every Sunday.

**Buckner would change that immediately.** You can't double the edge when the man inside is already in the quarterback's lap.

**And this ties straight into what Kubiak admitted about time to pressure this week** — that the deeper you get into a season, the more tape teams have, and the longer it takes to get home. **Rob Leonard was standing at that podium asking out loud whether they can rush four and get there.** An interior rusher is the answer to that question.

**The catch: Buckner is also an impending free agent, and he's 32. Grover Stewart is 32 as well.** These are **rentals.** The Raiders would have to decide whether paying real money on a second contract makes sense for a team still building. **If they can be had for a late-round pick, great. If not, I pass.**

**And one piece of real credit where it's due: Kwity Paye had a sack in the Bills game.** We've been hard on that contract on this channel — **$48 million and no production for five weeks.** He finally got home. **I'll give him that.**
